# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Command media mathematics through core vocabulary (CPM foundations
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Media Mathematics & Algorithmic Targeting scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** command media mathematics through core vocabulary (CPM foundations, performance rates, earned-paid distinctions, viewability hierarchy), reach-frequency math (relationships, effective frequency, rating points, overlap algebra), budget arithmetic (working-media splits, pacing math, diminishing returns, break-even computation), funnel systems (conversion algebra, volume-quality tradeoffs, attribution windows, incrementality adjustment), comparability discipline (definition audits, apples-to-oranges guards, blended modeling), and numeracy habits (back-of-envelope fluency, unit consistency, sanity checks), avoiding impression equality assumptions, reach addition errors, fee blindness, single-stage optimization, cross-platform naivety, and tool-dependent numeracy failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.
