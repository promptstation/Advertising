# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Govern bidding automation through strategy taxonomy (manual-automated spectrum
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Media Mathematics & Algorithmic Targeting scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** govern bidding automation through strategy taxonomy (manual-automated spectrum, objective families, selection judgment, portfolio application), smart-bidding machinery (auction-time prediction, conversion models, signal dependency, learning phases), pacing systems (pacing algorithms, budget interactions, frequency capping, seasonality adaptation), governance practice (value-signal engineering, guardrail monitoring, intervention timing, incrementality verification), the automation frontier (full-funnel systems, predictive allocation, AI agents, cross-platform coordination), and human-machine division (control allocation, behavior literacy, override discipline), avoiding objective mismatch, phase sabotage, window optimization, guardrail absence, funnel surrender, and manual romanticism failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone

**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
