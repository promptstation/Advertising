## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the search channel whose intent-driven auctions monetize commercial queries at the highest efficiency in digital media, and whose mechanics illuminate auction economics in general: the search-ecosystem foundation (the query-intent-spectrum where navigational, informational, and transactional queries carry different commercial value, structuring keyword strategy per the intent literatures; the auction-participant-landscape where the dominant search platform, secondary engines, and marketplace search systems operate variant auction rules per the channel literatures; the quality-score-architecture where predicted click-through rate, ad relevance, and landing-page experience combine with bid into ad rank, the mechanism rewarding alignment over pure spend per the platform-documentation traditions of the sources; the position-economics reality where ad rank determines placement whose click-distribution curve makes position a nonlinear value variable per the position literatures); the keyword-system literacy (the match-type-mechanics where exact, phrase, and broad matching control query eligibility with the broad-match-plus-smart-bidding combination whose expansion behavior demands monitoring per the matching literatures; the keyword-portfolio-architecture where brand, generic, competitor, long-tail, and category terms organize by intent, competition, and margin per the structure traditions; the negative-keyword-discipline where exclusion lists prevent budget leakage into irrelevant queries, the hygiene whose neglect silently degrades efficiency per the operations literatures; the query-mining-practice where search-term reports reveal actual triggering queries informing continuous portfolio refinement per the optimization traditions); the ad-and-extension craft (the responsive-search-ad mechanics where asset combinations get algorithmically assembled and tested with the asset-quality and coverage disciplines per the platform literatures; the extension-and-asset-system where sitelinks, callouts, structured snippets, and location assets expand presence with the eligibility rules documented per the extension literatures; the ad-copy-relevance-engineering where query-term alignment affects both quality score and click behavior connecting to the fluency principles of the neuromarketing companion; the policy-compliance-discipline where trademark, health, financial, and prohibited-content rules bound permissible advertising per the policy literatures); the shopping-and-visual-commerce layer (the product-feed-architecture where structured data quality — titles, attributes, images, availability, pricing — determines shopping-campaign eligibility and performance per the feed literatures; the feed-optimization-craft where title construction, attribute completeness, and segmentation drive auction participation per the shopping traditions; the retail-search-convergence where marketplace search advertising operates parallel systems per the retail-media section); the search-measurement discipline (the brand-versus-generic-incrementality question where brand-search campaigns claim conversions that would have occurred organically, requiring the incrementality testing of the analytics companion per the brand-bidding literatures; the search-attribution-entanglement where last-click search credit absorbs upper-funnel demand creation effects, the entanglement the attribution frameworks address per the measurement literatures; the auction-insights-interpretation where impression share, overlap, and outranking metrics reveal competitive dynamics with the platform-data-reliability caveats per the competitive literatures; the offline-conversion-integration where CRM and revenue data flow back into bidding signals per the signals section); and the search-strategy integration (the funnel-role-definition where search serves demand capture while display and social serve demand creation, the division whose budget wars ignore the complementary dynamics per the strategy literatures; the competitor-response-planning where auction dynamics shift with competitive entry, exit, and aggressiveness requiring monitoring per the game-theory literatures of the auction traditions; the seasonal-and-event-planning where query volume cycles and competitive intensity shifts shape budget timing per the planning traditions of the strategy section; the AI-search-disruption-awareness where conversational and AI-mediated search interfaces reshape query behavior and ad placement with the strategic-response planning emerging per the frontier literatures) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the broad-match-unmonitored spender whose automated matching expansion bleeds budget into irrelevant queries because search-term review never happens, remediated by the query-mining discipline; the quality-score-ignorer whose high bids fight poor relevance and landing experience, overpaying for positions alignment would earn cheaper, remediated by the architecture literacy; the brand-bidding-assumer whose brand-term spend faces no incrementality test, potentially buying organic conversions, remediated by the measurement discipline; the negative-keyword-neglecter whose exclusion hygiene decay silently taxes every campaign, remediated by the discipline practice; the feed-neglect-shopping-runner whose product-data poverty suppresses auction participation regardless of bid, remediated by the feed architecture; and the last-click-imperialist whose search-favoring attribution conclusions starve the demand-creation channels feeding search volume, remediated by the funnel-role integration with detection methods as the diagnostic.
