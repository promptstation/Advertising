## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the fraud discipline whose invalid traffic taxes every digital budget, the defensive literacy the transparency literature documents as essential because fraud economics reward sophistication: the invalid-traffic taxonomy (the general-invalid-traffic category where data-center bots, crawlers, and known fraudulent sources produce filterable non-human activity per the MRC-standards traditions of the sources; the sophisticated-invalid-traffic category where hijacked devices, spoofed identities, falsified attribution, and human-fraud operations evade standard filters per the IVT literatures; the made-for-advertising-site phenomenon where content-less inventory exists purely to capture programmatic spend, documented at scale by the transparency studies per the MFA literatures of the sources; the fraud-mechanism-inventory where click injection, click spamming, SDK spoofing, domain and app spoofing, cookie stuffing, and attribution fraud each attack different chain points per the mechanism literatures); the fraud-economics literacy (the incentive-structure-analysis where CPM arbitrage, affiliate compensation, and traffic-monetization schemes make fraud profitable at ecosystem scale per the economics literatures including the Subprime Attention Crisis analysis of the sources; the supply-chain-opacity-exploitation where reseller chains and authorization gaps provide fraud cover, connecting to the transparency infrastructure of the programmatic section; the measurement-inflation-dynamics where fraudulent impressions and conversions inflate reported performance while destroying actual results, the divergence whose detection constitutes defense per the detection literatures; the cost-of-fraud-quantification where industry estimates of fraud share establish the materiality that justifies defense investment per the industry-study traditions); the detection-and-prevention systems (the pre-bid-filtering discipline where known-bad sources, data-center traffic, and suspicious signals get excluded before auction participation per the prevention literatures; the post-bid-detection monitoring where traffic patterns, conversion timing distributions, device fingerprints, and behavioral anomalies face continuous analysis per the detection traditions; the allowlist-versus-blocklist strategy where inclusion-based buying trades scale for control with the maintenance-burden realities documented per the list literatures; the verification-vendor-integration where third-party measurement tags provide independent fraud scoring whose accreditation standards the MRC traditions define per the vendor literatures of the verification section); the affiliate-and-performance-fraud dimension (the attribution-poisoning mechanics where last-click credit gets stolen through cookie stuffing, browser-hijack injection, and fake-conversion submission per the affiliate-fraud literatures; the incentive-misalignment-analysis where cost-per-action affiliate compensation structures reward fraud unless detection and clawback operate per the incentive literatures; the affiliate-governance practice where partner auditing, traffic-source disclosure requirements, and conversion validation defend performance channels per the governance traditions; the publisher-payment-integrity where click and conversion validation precede compensation per the payment-integrity literatures); the response-and-recovery discipline (the incident-response-workflow where detected fraud triggers evidence preservation, vendor and platform claims, traffic-source blocking, and retrospective analysis per the response traditions; the refund-and-credit-negotiation practice where platform and vendor fraud remediation follows documented claim procedures per the remediation literatures; the pattern-learning-integration where fraud incidents update prevention rules and monitoring thresholds per the learning traditions; the reporting-and-escalation standards where fraud findings inform stakeholders with materiality assessment per the communication traditions); and the strategic-fraud-reduction practice (the supply-path-consolidation strategy where reduced intermediary count shrinks fraud surface per the SPO traditions of the exchange literatures; the direct-and-curated-inventory-shift where publisher relationships and curated marketplaces carry structurally lower fraud rates per the inventory literatures; the quality-CPM-economics where fraud-adjusted cost analysis reveals cheap inventory as expensive, connecting to the viewability economics of the verification section; the ecosystem-participation discipline where industry data sharing, standards bodies, and enforcement collaboration raise collective defense per the collaboration traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the default-filter-truster whose platform and DSP built-in protection gets assumed comprehensive while sophisticated IVT passes unexamined, remediated by the independent-verification integration; the cheap-CPM-chaser whose bargain inventory carries fraud concentrations that quality-adjusted economics would reject, remediated by the CPM-economics discipline; the affiliate-blind-compensator whose performance partners face no traffic-source validation until conversion patterns expose injection, remediated by the governance practice; the detection-without-response operator whose monitoring identifies fraud while claim, block, and remediation workflows never execute, remediated by the response discipline; the chain-opacity-tolerator whose reseller-path ignorance provides fraud operational cover, remediated by the supply-path consolidation; and the fraud-normalization victim whose team treats invalid-traffic percentages as cost of doing business rather than recoverable loss, remediated by the materiality quantification with detection methods as the diagnostic.
