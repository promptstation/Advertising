## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the verification discipline whose independent measurement distinguishes delivered value from reported volume: the brand-safety-and-suitability framework (the safety-versus-suitability distinction where categorical exclusion of harmful content differs from brand-specific contextual appropriateness judgment per the suitability literatures; the content-classification-systems where inventory categorization by topic, sentiment, and risk level enables control with the classification-accuracy limitations documented per the taxonomy literatures; the control-mechanism-inventory where keyword exclusion, category blocking, inclusion lists, and verification-vendor enforcement operate with the over-blocking collateral damage requiring balance per the control literatures; the adjacency-judgment discipline where news and controversial-context adjacency faces brand-specific assessment rather than blanket exclusion whose reach costs the industry documents per the adjacency literatures); the viewability-measurement system (the MRC-viewability-standards where display and video impressions count as viewable under pixel-percentage and duration thresholds per the standards traditions of the sources; the viewability-economics-connection where unviewed impressions carry zero message-transfer value making viewable-CPM the honest efficiency metric per the economics literatures; the placement-and-format-determinants where position, page layout, and ad format drive viewability rates structurally per the determinant literatures; the attention-measurement-evolution where dwell time, gaze-based, and physiological attention metrics extend beyond binary viewability with the standardization and vendor-claim scrutiny emerging per the attention literatures connecting to the neuromarketing companion); the verification-vendor landscape (the independent-measurement-principle where third parties unaffiliated with transacting parties provide credibility whose accreditation the MRC framework establishes per the independence literatures; the vendor-capability-comparison where measurement coverage, methodology, platform-integration depth, and discrepancy patterns differ across providers per the vendor literatures; the platform-versus-third-party-discrepancy management where reported numbers diverge systematically requiring reconciliation methodology and cause understanding per the discrepancy literatures; the measurement-cost-benefit-analysis where verification spend faces the value of detected problems and negotiation leverage per the economics traditions); the quality-reporting-and-accountability practice (the delivery-quality-dashboard discipline where viewability, IVT, brand-safety incidents, and discrepancy rates receive continuous reporting per the monitoring traditions; the contract-and-remediation-frameworks where quality guarantees, make-good provisions, and payment conditions encode standards commercially per the contract literatures; the publisher-and-partner-accountability where quality performance informs sourcing decisions and relationship terms per the accountability traditions; the industry-standards-participation where measurement evolution through bodies and working groups shapes future practice per the standards traditions); the cross-channel-verification challenges (the social-platform-verification-limits where walled-garden measurement access restrictions constrain independent audit per the platform literatures of the ecosystem section; the connected-TV-and-emerging-channel measurement where new channels develop verification capability unevenly per the emerging literatures of the channels section; the audio-and-podcast-verification where download-based and stream-based measurement traditions differ per the audio literatures; the retail-media-measurement-closure where commerce platforms measure their own inventory with sales data advantage and independence deficit per the retail literatures of the channels section); and the verification-strategy discipline (the risk-proportional-measurement design where verification intensity matches spend materiality and channel risk profile per the strategy literatures; the triangulation-architecture where platform reports, third-party measurement, and business outcomes cross-validate per the triad traditions of the analytics companion; the continuous-improvement-loop where verification findings feed sourcing, targeting, and creative decisions rather than accumulating as reports per the improvement traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the impression-count-believer whose delivery reports never distinguish served from viewable from attentive, paying for invisible advertising, remediated by the viewability economics; the over-blocking-defender whose thousand-keyword exclusions destroy reach while missing semantic nuance, remediated by the adjacency-judgment discipline; the discrepancy-paralyzed analyst whose platform and vendor numbers conflict for months without reconciliation methodology, remediated by the discrepancy management; the verification-absent-spender whose emerging-channel budgets run on platform self-report alone, remediated by the triangulation architecture; the dashboard-collector whose quality metrics accumulate without feeding sourcing and contract decisions, remediated by the improvement loop; and the safety-suitability-conflater whose blanket category exclusions forfeit brand-appropriate reach that suitability judgment would capture, remediated by the framework distinction with detection methods as the diagnostic.
