the shoppable-and-interactive-video-emergence where convergence with commerce creates new response mechanics per the convergence literatures); the audio-and-podcast channel (the streaming-audio-programmatic mechanics where music and radio inventory trades with contextual and demographic targeting per the audio literatures; the digital-out-of-home and place-based media (the DOOH-programmatic-mechanics where screen networks trade through demand-side platforms with location and daypart targeting per the OOH literatures; the measurement-approach development where audience estimation, mobile-panel correlation, and sales-lift studies substitute for impression tracking per the measurement literatures; the place-based-network-inventory where in-store, transit, and venue screens offer context-specific reach per the network literatures; the mobile-location-integration where device-mobility data connects exposure to visit outcomes with the privacy constraints per the location literatures of the analytics companion); the emerging-format landscape (the gaming-and-metaverse-adjacent inventory where in-game advertising and virtual environments develop commercial mechanics with the audience-receptivity questions per the gaming literatures; the AI-surface-emergence where conversational assistants and generated-content environments create new placement questions whose commercial forms remain unsettled per the frontier literatures; the creator-economy-integration where platform-native creator content merges with paid amplification per the creator literatures; the format-lifecycle-awareness where channels pass through early-advantage, standardization, and commoditization phases whose position determines investment timing per the lifecycle traditions); and the frontier-channel discipline (the measurement-parity-requirement where emerging channels face the verification standards of the established ecosystem rather than pioneer excuses per the verification traditions; the test-before-scale-methodology where new-channel investment follows structured pilot programs with incrementality measurement per the experimentation traditions of the analytics companion; the capability-development-judgment where channel expertise through specialists, training, and pilot experience precedes budget commitment per the capability traditions; the portfolio-integration practice where frontier channels enter media plans through the funnel-role and budget methodologies of the planning section rather than novelty allocation) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the closed-loop-circular-reasoner whose retail-media attribution credits purchases the platform would have made anyway without incrementality testing, remediated by the test-before-scale methodology;
