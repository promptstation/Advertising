the mobile-first-design-discipline where the dominant traffic source's constraints govern layout, input, and hierarchy per the mobile literatures); the conversion-rate-optimization practice (the hypothesis-driven-testing-program where behavioral-mechanism hypotheses from the companion course drive structured experimentation with the statistical standards of the analytics companion per the CRO literatures; the fatigue-management-discipline where creative refresh cycles respond to frequency-driven decay per the wear-out literatures of the behavioral companion); the commerce-and-catalog integration (the product-page-optimization where merchandising content, price presentation, and availability signals convert retail traffic per the e-commerce literatures connecting to the pricing psychology of the behavioral companion;
