# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Understand social algorithms through ranking architecture (candidate-ranking pipelines
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Media Mathematics & Algorithmic Targeting scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** understand social algorithms through ranking architecture (candidate-ranking pipelines, signal weighting, personalization, exploration-exploitation), the paid-organic interface (auction integration, reach economics, earned amplification, cross-subsidy strategy), platform literacy (Meta, TikTok, YouTube, LinkedIn mechanics, commonality extraction), creative interaction (thumb-stopping requirements, bait boundaries, format-native design, fatigue dynamics), feedback systems (learning phases, signal dependency, audience expansion, diagnostic reading), and adaptation discipline (change resilience, first-party leverage, testing institutionalization), avoiding algorithm blaming, learning thrashing, engagement baiting, platform dependence, organic-paid confusion, and signal starvation failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.
