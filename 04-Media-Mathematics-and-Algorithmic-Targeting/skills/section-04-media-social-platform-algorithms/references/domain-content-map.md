## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the algorithmic systems whose ranking decisions determine whether creative reaches audiences at all, the social-media-algorithm understanding the course description names as core engineering knowledge: the ranking-system architecture (the candidate-generation-and-ranking pipeline where billions of daily posts narrow through retrieval, ranking, and policy-filtering stages per the platform-engineering literatures of the sources; the signal-weighting reality where predicted engagement probabilities across reactions, comments, shares, dwell time, and negative feedback combine into ranking scores whose weights platforms adjust continuously per the ranking literatures; the personalization-mechanics where user history, relationship graph, and behavior patterns customize feeds making identical posts reach different audiences per the recommendation literatures; the exploration-exploitation-balance where algorithmic testing of new content against established favorites creates the distribution volatility creators and brands experience per the bandit-algorithm traditions); the paid-versus-organic interface (the ad-auction-integration where advertising bids compete within ranking systems under the total-value formulations combining bid, predicted engagement, and ad-quality scores per the platform-documentation traditions of the sources; the organic-reach-economics where declining unpaid distribution made paid amplification structurally necessary, the business-model reality shaping social strategy per the reach literatures; the earned-amplification-dynamics where high organic engagement improves paid delivery efficiency through quality-score mechanisms per the interaction literatures; the cross-subsidy-strategy craft where organic community building and paid distribution reinforce through audience asset accumulation per the social-strategy traditions); the platform-specific-system literacy (the Meta-family mechanics where feed, Reels, and Stories placements operate distinct ranking systems with the Advantage-suite automation whose signal dependencies the bidding section details per the platform documentation; the TikTok-recommendation-system where interest-graph distribution prioritizes content affinity over social connection, creating rapid viral potential and equally rapid decay per the recommendation literatures; the YouTube-and-video-ranking where watch-time prediction, session contribution, and thumbnail-click dynamics govern distribution per the video literatures; the LinkedIn-and-professional-context mechanics where relevance scoring operates within network and content norms per the platform traditions; the algorithm-commonality-extraction where engagement prediction, freshness, relationship weight, and content-quality signals recur across platforms despite implementation differences per the cross-platform literatures); the creative-algorithm interaction (the thumb-stopping-requirement where scroll-velocity competition makes the first moments of creative functionally part of targeting per the attention literatures of the neuromarketing companion; the engagement-bait-boundary where explicit interaction solicitation faces algorithmic demotion and policy penalty, distinguishing legitimate engagement design from manipulation per the policy literatures; the format-native-design discipline where vertical video, sound-off legibility, and platform-convention adherence affect distribution because ranking systems learn format performance patterns per the creative literatures of the creative companion course; the frequency-and-fatigue-dynamics where repeated exposure degrades response and triggers negative-feedback signals that suppress delivery per the wear-out literatures of the behavioral companion); the optimization-feedback systems (the learning-phase-mechanics where delivery systems require conversion volume to stabilize optimization, the cold-start dynamics whose violation through constant editing resets learning per the platform-documentation traditions; the signal-dependency-principle where algorithm performance inherits conversion-signal quality, making tracking infrastructure the foundation of automated optimization per the signals section; the audience-expansion-behavior where algorithmic delivery drifts beyond defined targeting toward predicted-value users with the control-versus-performance tradeoff documented per the expansion literatures; the diagnostic-reading-practice where delivery metrics — impression share, frequency curves, cost trends, audience overlap — reveal algorithm behavior for intervention per the analytics traditions); and the strategy-adaptation discipline (the algorithm-change-resilience where strategy diversification across formats, platforms, and owned channels buffers unilateral ranking changes per the resilience traditions; the first-party-audience-leverage where customer lists and engaged-audience signals improve algorithmic delivery within privacy-era constraints per the data literatures of the analytics companion; the testing-institutionalization where creative and audience hypotheses face continuous platform experimentation per the optimization section) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the algorithm-blamer whose distribution failures trace to creative weakness and signal poverty rather than ranking persecution, remediated by the diagnostic-reading practice; the learning-phase-thrasher whose daily campaign edits reset optimization continuously, guaranteeing instability, remediated by the phase-mechanics literacy; the engagement-bait-operator whose interaction solicitation draws demotion and policy action while poisoning audience quality, remediated by the boundary literacy; the single-platform-dependent strategist whose entire funnel inherits one ranking adjustment as existential crisis, remediated by the resilience discipline; the organic-paid-confused planner whose strategy assumes reach economics the platform business model ended years ago, remediated by the reach-economics literacy; and the signal-starved-automator whose smart systems receive sparse, delayed, or corrupted conversion events, optimizing toward noise, remediated by the dependency principle with detection methods as the diagnostic.
