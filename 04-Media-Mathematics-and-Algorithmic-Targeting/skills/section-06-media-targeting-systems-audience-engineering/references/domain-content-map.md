## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the targeting machinery whose audience construction the course description names as the mechanism ensuring every dollar reaches the user most likely to convert: the targeting-dimension inventory (the demographic-and-geographic layer where age, gender, location, and language filters operate with the platform-accuracy caveats on inferred attributes documented per the targeting literatures; the interest-and-affinity layer where behavioral inference categorizes users by content consumption and activity patterns with the inference-error rates that demand performance validation per the interest literatures; the intent-and-life-event layer where recent search and activity signals indicate active consideration states per the intent literatures; the custom-audience layer where advertiser-supplied lists and engagement-based segments deliver first-party precision per the data literatures of the analytics companion; the contextual-layer renaissance where content and environment classification targets without user identification per the contextual traditions of the programmatic section); the audience-asset architecture (the first-party-foundation discipline where CRM lists, site engagement, purchase history, and app activity constitute the durable targeting assets whose collection and activation the privacy frameworks govern per the data-strategy literatures; the lookalike-and-similarity-expansion mechanics where seed audiences generate predicted-similar populations with the seed-quality dependency, platform-opacity, and privacy-era constraint documentation per the expansion literatures; the audience-tiering-strategy where retention, cross-sell, warm-prospect, and cold-acquisition tiers receive differentiated messaging and bids per the funnel traditions connecting to the customer-value frameworks of the analytics companion; the suppression-and-exclusion discipline where existing customers, recent converters, and irrelevant segments get excluded preventing waste and irritation per the hygiene traditions); the privacy-era-targeting transformation (the identifier-degradation-reality where cookie deprecation, app-tracking restrictions, and consent requirements compress observable audience signals per the transformation literatures of the analytics companion; the consented-data-primacy shift where zero-party and first-party data with explicit permission replace inferred tracking as targeting foundation per the consent literatures; the platform-model-compensation where machine-learning delivery systems substitute predicted-value optimization for explicit targeting parameters, shifting control from buyer-defined audiences to algorithm-defined ones per the automation literatures of the bidding section; the clean-room-and-collaboration-architecture where privacy-preserving matching enables audience intersection without raw data sharing per the clean-room literatures of the analytics companion; the contextual-and-creative-targeting-rebalancing where environment selection and message relevance absorb targeting burden formerly carried by user identification per the rebalancing literatures); the targeting-performance discipline (the audience-quality-assessment where segment response rates, conversion values, and incrementality face measurement rather than assumed precision per the validation traditions; the scale-versus-precision-tradeoff analysis where narrow targeting raises relevance while restricting volume and raising costs through auction competition, with the optimal-breadth mathematics per the tradeoff literatures; the overlap-and-frequency-management where multi-campaign audiences intersect causing internal competition and excessive frequency per the coordination traditions; the exclusion-logic-verification where suppression rules actually execute because platform hierarchies and update delays break intended exclusions per the operations literatures); the ethical-and-compliance-targeting boundaries (the sensitive-category-restrictions where housing, employment, credit, health, and political advertising face regulatory limits on audience selection per the enforcement literatures of the analytics companion's ethics frameworks; the proxy-discrimination-awareness where behavioral targeting can achieve exclusionary outcomes without explicit sensitive attributes per the fairness literatures of the analytics companion; the vulnerability-protection-discipline where targeting toward financially distressed, addiction-affected, or crisis-state audiences crosses the ethical lines of the behavioral companion per the vulnerability literatures; the transparency-obligations where political-ad libraries, targeting disclosure requirements, and platform policy establish public accountability per the transparency traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the precision-illusionist whose micro-targeted segments rest on inferred attributes of unvalidated accuracy, buying confidence rather than conversion, remediated by the quality-assessment discipline; the lookalike-launderer whose similarity expansion from polluted seed audiences propagates waste at scale, remediated by the seed-quality literacy; the identifier-era-fossil whose audience strategy awaits tracking capabilities that privacy transformation permanently removed, remediated by the transformation literacy; the overlap-blind-multiplier whose campaign structures auction against themselves for identical users at inflated frequency and cost, remediated by the management discipline; the suppression-failure victim whose existing customers receive acquisition offers because exclusion logic silently broke, remediated by the verification practice; and the boundary-pushing-targeter whose sensitive-category and vulnerability-adjacent audience construction draws enforcement and press exposure, remediated by the ethics discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
