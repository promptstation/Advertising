## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the motivation neuroscience whose reward circuits power desire, craving, and engagement-loop design — and whose exploitation risks define a central ethics frontier: the reward-system architecture (the mesolimbic-circuitry literacy where dopaminergic pathways from midbrain to striatum and prefrontal regions implement incentive salience, making reward-associated cues themselves motivating per the reward literatures of the brain section; the prediction-error-learning mechanism where outcomes versus expectation update cue value, the computational core of learned desire whose commercial expression includes surprise mechanics and expectation management per the reinforcement-learning traditions; the incentive-salience concept where cues acquire magnetic attention-grabbing and approach-triggering properties through association, explaining why packaging, notifications, and brand stimuli provoke desire independent of conscious wanting per the salience literatures); the wanting-liking dissociation (the Berridge-distinction principle where wanting — incentive motivation driving pursuit — and liking — hedonic pleasure in consumption — run on separable systems, producing the documented patterns of desire without enjoyment and enjoyment without desire per the affective-neuroscience traditions; the addiction-model-insight where compulsive pursuit despite negative consequences reflects wanting-system hijacking with liking decline, the pattern whose commercial mimicry defines the exploitation boundary per the addiction literatures; the consumption-design implication where satisfaction engineering and desire engineering are distinct crafts — liking governs retention and word of mouth while wanting governs pursuit and impulse — demanding both per the experience-design traditions); the commercial-application systems (the anticipation-and-teaser engineering where delayed reveal, countdown mechanics, and partial information exploit prediction and incentive-salience systems per the launch literatures connecting to the scarcity section of the companion course; the variable-reward mechanics where unpredictable rewards in loyalty programs, gamified apps, and promotional games produce the strongest engagement persistence per the schedule literatures; the collection-and-completion drives where set completion, progress mechanics, and achievement systems engage documented goal-gradient and completion motivation per the gamification literatures of the retention companion section; the craving-cycle design in food, beverage, and habit categories where sensory-specific satiety management, flavor architecture, and cue exposure structure repeat desire per the appetite literatures; the social-reward integration where likes, shares, and status recognition exploit social-evaluation reward circuitry per the social-media literatures); the notification-and-attention economy (the interruption-response dynamics where alerts trigger orienting responses whose suppression costs cognitive effort, making notification design an attention-capture system per the interruption literatures; the compulsion-loop patterns where checking behavior reinforced by intermittent social and content rewards builds automatic engagement per the habit-loop connection of the retention section; the design-responsibility debate where engagement optimization and wellbeing conflict drives platform regulation, design-ethics movements, and the attention-rights discussion per the tech-ethics literatures); the measurement discipline (the approach-motivation measurement where frontal-asymmetry, pupil dilation, reaction-time approach tendencies, and behavioral pursuit metrics index wanting with the tool-reliability caveats of the measurement section; the liking-assessment methods where consumption-phase facial response, self-report, and repeat-choice behavior measure enjoyment separately from pursuit per the dissociation-testing traditions; the loop-behavior-analytics where engagement patterns, session structure, and return dynamics reveal operative reward mechanics in production systems per the product-analytics literatures); and the exploitation-ethics frontier (the dark-engagement taxonomy where variable-reward harvesting, streak-anxiety mechanics, infinite-scroll absence of stopping cues, and regret-minimization traps constitute the manipulation patterns regulators and ethicists catalog per the dark-pattern literatures of the companion course; the vulnerability-dimension where developing brains, addiction histories, and impulse-control difficulties face heightened harm from reward-loop design per the vulnerability literatures; the sustainable-engagement principle where loops serving genuine value produce retention without resentment, while extraction loops accumulate churn, regulation, and reputational cost per the long-term-value traditions; the regulatory-horizon awareness where gambling-adjacency concerns in loot-box mechanics, minor-protection rules, and digital-wellbeing legislation reshape permissible design per the regulation-tracking disciplines) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the loop-extractor whose engagement mechanics pursue wanting-hijacking patterns that generate usage metrics alongside growing resentment and regulatory attention, remediated by the sustainable-engagement principle; the liking-neglector whose desire-engineered campaigns drive trial that consumption experience fails to retain, remediated by the dissociation literacy; the variable-reward-casino operator whose gambling-adjacent mechanics attract minor-protection and loot-box enforcement, remediated by the regulatory-horizon awareness; the notification-spammer whose interruption volume captures attention short-term while training systematic app dismissal, remediated by the interruption-response literacy; the unmeasured-craving-designer whose reward mechanics deploy without approach-motivation or loop analytics verification, remediated by the measurement discipline; and the autonomy-dismissing optimizer whose engagement science treats user wellbeing as externality until platform policy or press forces reckoning, remediated by the exploitation-ethics frontier with detection methods as the diagnostic.
