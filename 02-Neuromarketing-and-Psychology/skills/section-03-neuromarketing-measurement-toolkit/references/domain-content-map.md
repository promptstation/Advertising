## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the instrumentation whose capabilities, costs, and limitations determine which tool answers which question, the methodological literacy that protects budgets from tool fascination: the method-comparison framework (the resolution tradeoff axis where electroencephalography delivers millisecond timing with weak spatial localization while functional magnetic resonance imaging delivers regional localization with seconds of lag and scanner-artificial conditions per the neuroimaging literatures; the ecological-validity axis where measurement invasiveness inversely tracks environment realism, positioning eye tracking and wearables closest to natural exposure; the cost-tier reality spanning free platform analytics through mid-range biometric labs to seven-figure scanner studies, demanding the question-value match of the fitness-for-purpose discipline; the signal-quality hierarchy where behavioral outcomes remain the validity anchor that all physiological signals must ultimately correlate with per the measurement-validation traditions); the eye-tracking system (the fixation-and-saccade foundation where gaze measurement produces fixation counts, durations, and sequences revealing attention allocation across ad elements, shelves, and interfaces per the eye-tracking literatures; the derived-product suite including heatmaps, scanpaths, area-of-interest analysis, and time-to-first-fixation metrics whose interpretation requires the artifact-awareness that gaze does not equal processing or persuasion per the interpretation literatures; the deployment range where screen-based units serve digital and video testing, wearable glasses serve retail and shelf studies, and webcam approximation serves scale screening with reduced precision; the standard applications including package-design comparison, print-ad hierarchy verification, landing-page attention audit, and shelf-standout testing per the applied literatures of the channels section); the EEG and neural-timing system (the component-architecture literacy where event-related potentials including the P300 attention-and-memory allocation marker and the N400 semantic-incongruity marker index processing stages, with the frontal-asymmetry approach-withdrawal measure whose commercial use exceeds its reliability evidence per the EEG literatures; the practical constraints where electrical noise, movement artifact, individual skull variation, and setup burden limit stimulus freedom and sample throughput; the vendor-claim scrutiny discipline where headline engagement and attention scores compress contested science into black-box numbers requiring methodological transparency per the buying-guides of the practice section); the fMRI and deep-imaging system (the BOLD-mechanism literacy where blood-oxygen response proxies neural activity with seconds of delay, enabling regional attribution including the valuation, reward, and aversion findings of the brain section per the neuroimaging traditions; the artificiality problem where supine scanner viewing of brief stimuli with button-press responses generalizes poorly to living-room and shelf contexts; the appropriate-use niche where mechanism questions about brand effects on taste experience and price processing justify scanner cost per the landmark-study traditions); the biometric suite (the galvanic-skin-response measure where sweat-gland activity indexes arousal intensity without valence, requiring pairing with valence measures per the electrodermal literatures; the facial-coding systems where automated and manual muscle-movement classification reads expressed emotion with the cultural-display-rule and suppression caveats; the heart-rate and heart-rate-variability measures where cardiac patterns distinguish orienting, attention, and emotional engagement per the psychophysiology traditions; the combined-panel practice where arousal, valence, and attention measures triangulate because no single channel carries all three per the multimodal literatures); the implicit-measurement family (the reaction-time and association tests including implicit-attitude paradigms where response latencies reveal automatic evaluations that surveys miss, with the reliability and interpretation debates documented per the implicit-measurement literatures; the choice-based-conjoint and behavioral proxies where revealed preference under time pressure approximates automatic processing; the survey-plus-latency hybrids whose administration costs remain accessible to most teams per the tooling-tier reality); and the toolkit-integration practice (the question-to-method-mapping discipline where attention questions select eye tracking, arousal questions select biometrics, timing questions select EEG, mechanism questions select fMRI, and association questions select implicit tests per the mapping traditions; the triangulation-design practice where two or three methods plus behavior jointly constrain interpretation; the quality-standards literacy where sample sizes, baseline correction, artifact rejection, and statistical discipline determine whether any instrument produces knowledge per the methodology literatures of the practice section) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the gaze-equates-belief reader whose heatmap conclusions ignore that fixation does not guarantee encoding or persuasion, remediated by the interpretation literacy; the black-box-score buyer whose vendor engagement indices face no methodological audit before steering budget, remediated by the scrutiny discipline; the scanner-glamour-purchaser whose fMRI study answers a question eye tracking addressed for one percent of the cost, remediated by the question-to-method mapping; the single-channel-biometrician whose arousal spikes get read as excitement when fear and frustration produce identical conductance, remediated by the triangulation design; the artifact-blind analyst whose EEG conclusions rest on blink and movement contamination, remediated by the quality-standards literacy; and the tool-collection builder whose capability acquisition precedes question inventory, producing impressive equipment and no decisions, remediated by the fitness-for-purpose discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
