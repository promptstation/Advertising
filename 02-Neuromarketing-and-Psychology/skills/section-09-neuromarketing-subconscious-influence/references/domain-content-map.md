# Domain Content Map — Subconscious Influence: Priming, Implicit Attitudes, and the Subliminal Myth Audit

development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Navigate automatic influence through the automaticity taxonomy (pre-attentive, supraliminal, subliminal, implicit tiers), priming evidence audits (robust findings, fragile inventory, mechanism distinctions, marketing applications), subliminal myth correction (myth catalog, regulatory history, persistence psychology, honest capability statements), the implicit-attitude system (association measurement, divergence interpretation, formation dynamics, controversy awareness).
2. Design application (context priming, association management, fluency exploitation, automaticity testing), and ethics boundaries (autonomy considerations, deception lines, vulnerable audiences, professional positioning), avoiding subliminal belief, replication ignorance, implicit overreading, autonomy dismissal, semantic slipping, and myth perpetuation failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the automatic-influence layer whose real effects and exaggerated myths demand equal precision, because the discipline's credibility lives in the gap between them: the automaticity taxonomy (the pre-attentive-processing tier where features register before conscious awareness per the perception sections, the genuine bottom of the influence stack; the supraliminal-prime tier where briefly or peripherally presented stimuli influence subsequent evaluation and behavior without focused attention, the tier where replicated commercial effects actually live per the priming literatures; the subliminal tier where below-threshold stimulation produces effects that are real in laboratory conditions but small, fragile, and commercially overclaimed per the subliminal-research literatures; the implicit-attitude tier where learned associations activate automatically and shape behavior outside endorsement, measurable and influenceable without conscious access per the implicit-cognition traditions); the priming-evidence audit (the robust-priming-findings where goal-priming through words and images shows documented effects on subsequent behavior within the replication-verified subset per the post-crisis literature assessment; the fragile-findings inventory where celebrated effects including certain age-walking and money-priming results failed replication, demanding the evidence-grading habits of the companion course; the mechanism-priming distinction where semantic and affective priming through association networks operates differently from behavioral-mimicry claims whose commercial translation remains weakest; the marketing-relevant-priming applications where category cues, need activation, and brand-association priming in advertising contexts carry the applied literature support including hunger-cue and goal-activation effects per the consumer-priming traditions); the subliminal-myth correction (the myth-catalog audit where hidden-message mind control, flashed-frame purchase commands, and backward-masking persuasion face the empirical record showing threshold-level effects far below commercial fantasy per the debunking literatures; the regulatory-historical context where subliminal-advertising prohibitions in broadcast regulation persist despite the weak-effect evidence, making the practice legally restricted regardless of efficacy per the regulation traditions; the persistence-explanation psychology where the myth survives through confirmation bias, vendor incentive, and the seductive power of invisible-influence narratives per the belief-maintenance literatures; the honest-capability-statement craft where practitioners articulate what automatic influence genuinely delivers — salience capture, fluency preference, association activation, implicit bias shaping — versus what it cannot, protecting budgets and credibility per the professional-communication traditions); the implicit-attitude system (the association-measurement methods where reaction-time paradigms and choice-based implicit tests reveal automatic brand evaluations that explicit surveys mask, with the reliability and stability caveats documented per the implicit-measurement literatures; the implicit-explicit-divergence interpretation where gaps between automatic and endorsed attitudes predict behavior under cognitive load, distraction, and time pressure per the dual-attitude traditions; the implicit-association-formation dynamics where repeated pairing, affective conditioning, and cultural exposure build automatic associations that advertising can shape within the bounds of existing structure per the conditioning literatures; the bias-and-sensitivity awareness where implicit measures carry method controversies including test-retest reliability and predictive-validity debates requiring cautious interpretation per the measurement-skepticism traditions); the design-application discipline (the context-priming practice where advertising environments, adjacent content, and situational cues get selected to activate relevant goals and needs before message exposure per the pre-suasion connections of the companion course; the association-management strategy where brand pairings with contexts, characters, causes, and categories accumulate implicit association structure per the brand-network section; the fluency-and-familiarity exploitation where the genuinely robust automatic effects of the perception and memory sections carry most practical influence load per the evidence-hierarchy discipline; the automaticity-testing methods where dual-task, time-pressure, and exposure-brief conditions verify that influence operates without deliberation per the experimental traditions); and the ethics-and-disclosure boundary (the autonomy-consideration principle where influence outside awareness raises distinct ethical questions because consumers cannot evaluate what they do not detect, per the manipulation literatures of the companion course; the deception-line discipline where concealed persuasion attempts including undisclosed native influence and disguised advocacy cross transparency standards even when effects are modest per the disclosure traditions; the vulnerable-audience protection where automatic-influence techniques applied to children and impaired-audience contexts face heightened restriction per the regulatory frameworks; the professional-positioning practice where subconscious-design knowledge serves better-fluency communication rather than covert manipulation, the sustainable identity of the discipline per the ethics traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the subliminal-believer whose budget chases flashed-message vendors selling myth against evidence, remediated by the myth-correction literacy; the replication-ignoring primer whose strategy cites pre-crisis findings that failed reproduction, remediated by the evidence-audit discipline; the implicit-overreader whose single implicit-test result gets treated as stable personality truth about audiences, remediated by the reliability caveats; the autonomy-dismissive influencer whose covert-technique enthusiasm ignores that undetectable influence carries distinct ethical weight, remediated by the boundary discipline; the semantic-slipper who uses subconscious loosely for anything attention-light, collapsing the taxonomy that makes claims testable, remediated by the automaticity tiers; and the myth-perpetuating presenter whose client decks recycle invisible-influence stories for persuasion drama, remediated by the honest-capability craft with detection methods as the diagnostic.
