## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the operational research discipline that turns instrumentation knowledge into valid, purchased, or conducted studies whose findings survive scrutiny: the study-design foundation (the question-to-design mapping where measurement objectives determine method selection per the toolkit section with explicit hypotheses stating expected signal differences; the stimulus-preparation craft where test materials control duration, order, context, and baseline conditions because uncontrolled stimuli contaminate physiological signal per the stimulus-design traditions; the control-and-baseline discipline where neutral baselines, counterbalanced ordering, and control stimuli isolate treatment effects from general arousal and order artifacts per the experimental traditions; the sample-size-and-population standards where physiological studies require the power analysis of the experimentation companion with attention to individual-variation noise per the methodology literatures); the data-collection operations (the environment-control requirements where lighting, sound, temperature, and electromagnetic interference receive management matched to instrument sensitivity per the lab-standards traditions; the participant-state-protocols where arrival conditions, instruction scripts, familiarization periods, and state questionnaires control the moderators documented in the brain section; the artifact-management discipline where blinks, movement, sweat anomalies, and equipment failures receive detection and handling procedures per the signal-processing literatures; the multi-method-synchronization practice where simultaneous eye tracking, biometrics, and response collection require timestamp alignment per the multimodal traditions); the analysis-and-interpretation discipline (the preprocessing-standards literacy where filtering, artifact rejection, baseline correction, and epoch selection decisions shape results before any statistics, demanding documentation per the processing traditions; the statistical-rigor requirements where physiological time-series analysis faces multiple-comparison, autocorrelation, and clustering challenges per the analysis literatures; the effect-size-and-meaning interpretation where statistical significance separates from commercial relevance per the evidence-grading habits; the convergent-validation practice where physiological findings face triangulation against behavior, self-report, and business metrics before conclusions per the triangulation standards of the foundations section); the vendor-buying literacy (the vendor-landscape assessment where full-service labs, platform providers, and specialists differ in methods, transparency, and quality per the market literatures; the methodology-interrogation checklist where proposals face questions on instruments, protocols, sample standards, analysis methods, baselines, artifact handling, and validation history per the buying-guide traditions including the NMSBA standards; the black-box-score-scrutiny where proprietary engagement and attention indices demand construct-validity evidence, reliability data, and benchmark documentation before acceptance per the score-literacy traditions; the deliverable-quality-assessment where reports must carry methods sections, limitation statements, raw-versus-processed clarity, and actionable recommendations rather than impressive graphics alone per the reporting standards); the in-house-capability building (the accessible-tooling pathway where webcam eye tracking, implicit tests, facial-coding platforms, and biometric wearables enable internal programs at moderate cost per the tooling-tier literatures; the skill-development requirements where operators need training in protocols, artifact recognition, and statistical interpretation before findings carry weight per the competency traditions; the hybrid-sourcing strategy where routine testing runs in-house while specialized questions commission expert labs per the sourcing disciplines; the knowledge-management systems where study protocols, results, and learning accumulate into institutional bases per the experimentation-program traditions of the companion course); the reporting-and-integration practice (the finding-translation craft where physiological results convert into design and strategy recommendations with mechanism explanation connecting signal to behavior per the communication traditions; the confidence-calibration reporting where study limitations, effect strengths, and validation status accompany conclusions per the uncertainty-communication standards; the decision-integration workflow where study outputs feed campaign development, creative iteration, and media planning through defined channels per the research-utilization traditions; the replication-and-tracking discipline where influential findings face confirmation studies and longitudinal validation per the scientific traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the stimulus-sloppy researcher whose uncontrolled materials make signal differences uninterpretable, remediated by the preparation craft; the artifact-blind collector whose motion and noise contamination passes as neural response, remediated by the management discipline; the preprocessing-opaque analyst whose filtering and rejection choices — the steps that most shape physiological results — go undocumented, remediated by the standards literacy; the vendor-glamour-buyer whose impressive lab tour substitutes for methodology interrogation, remediated by the checklist discipline; the score-surface-acceptor whose proprietary indices steer budget without construct-validity evidence, remediated by the scrutiny practice; and the shelf-ware-capability builder whose in-house instruments sit unused because operator skill and protocol development never accompanied purchase, remediated by the capability-building pathway with detection methods as the diagnostic.
