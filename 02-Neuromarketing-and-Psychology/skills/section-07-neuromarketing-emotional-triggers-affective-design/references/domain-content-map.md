## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the emotion discipline whose triggers the course description names as shaping purchasing before conscious realization, grounded in the effectiveness evidence that feeling drives commercial outcomes: the emotion-science foundation (the component-model literacy where emotional episodes combine physiological arousal, expressive behavior, subjective feeling, and action tendency, each measurable and each designable per the emotion literatures; the valence-arousal-circumplex framework where affective states map on pleasantness and activation dimensions, organizing stimulus effects from calming through exciting per the dimensional-model traditions; the discrete-emotion specificity principle where fear, sadness, anger, joy, guilt, and nostalgia carry different action tendencies — fear seeks safety, anger seeks confrontation, sadness seeks comfort and change, guilt seeks repair — making emotion selection a strategic decision rather than intensity maximization per the discrete-emotion literatures; the appraisal-antecedent system where emotions arise from evaluated relevance, goal-congruence, coping potential, and agency attribution, giving designers levers through situational construction per the appraisal traditions); the emotional-advertising evidence base (the effectiveness-databank findings where campaigns generating strong emotional response outperform rational-persuasion campaigns on business outcomes including the documented long-term profit-effect advantage per the IPA traditions of the foundations section; the emotion-plus-attention interaction where felt response amplifies encoding and sharing, connecting to the memory and viral literatures; the brand-affect-transfer mechanism where ad-evoked feelings condition toward the brand through association per the affect-transfer traditions; the wear-out-and-saturation dynamics where emotional intensity requires novelty maintenance per the repetition literatures of the companion course); the trigger catalog and craft (the joy-and-humor triggers where positive arousal drives sharing and affiliation with the comprehension-risk that jokes failing at the brand's expense damage rather than build per the humor literatures; the fear-and-threat triggers where protective motivation operates within the documented band between insufficient arousal and defensive denial, requiring efficacy-pairing that gives the viewer actionable response per the fear-appeal literatures; the sadness-and-empathy triggers whose helping motivation powers charitable and care-category communication per the prosocial literatures; the nostalgia-and-warmth triggers where sentimental recall of personal and cultural past builds connection and continuity association per the nostalgia literatures; the anger-and-injustice triggers where moral outrage drives engagement and alignment with the boundary conditions of polarization risk per the moral-emotion literatures; the awe-and-wonder triggers where vastness and admiration produce the documented sharing and time-perception effects per the self-transcendent-emotion literatures; the pride-and-accomplishment triggers where achievement association serves status and self-improvement categories per the self-conscious-emotion literatures); the design-implementation system (the narrative-transportation mechanism where story immersion suspends counter-arguing and produces emotion through identification and plot rather than direct appeal per the transportation literatures; the music-visual-verbal-emotion layering where modality contributions combine per the crossmodal foundations with the coherence discipline; the casting-and-face-response craft where expressions trigger documented mimicry and empathic response per the facial-feedback traditions; the pacing-and-peak engineering where emotional arc construction places intensity at memory-weighted positions per the peak-end connections of the companion course); the measurement-and-validation discipline (the affect-measurement toolkit where facial coding, skin conductance, self-report, and implicit measures assess emotional response per the measurement section with the arousal-valence disambiguation requirement; the emotion-behavior-link testing where felt response faces correlation with conversion, sharing, and brand metrics per the validation traditions; the individual-and-cultural-variation management where display rules, humor comprehension, and trigger sensitivity vary across audiences per the cross-cultural literatures of the anthropology companion); and the emotional-ethics boundary (the vulnerability-exploitation line where fear, guilt, and loneliness triggers applied to susceptible populations cross from persuasion into harm per the ethics traditions of the companion course; the authenticity requirement where brand-emotion pairing survives scrutiny of whether the brand can deliver on the feeling it evokes per the brand-integrity literatures) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the intensity-maximizer whose shock and sorrow escalations produce arousal without direction, activating viewers who never receive a behavioral channel, remediated by the action-tendency literacy; the efficacy-orphan fear-monger whose threat appeals lack coping response, triggering defensive avoidance rather than protection behavior, remediated by the fear-appeal band; the humor-gambler whose joke creative risks brand-cost failure modes without comprehension testing, remediated by the humor-risk discipline; the rational-only-defender whose briefs exclude emotional strategy despite the effectiveness-evidence advantage, remediated by the databank literacy; the emotion-brand-mismatcher whose warm campaigns front cold customer experiences, converting affect transfer into hypocrisy perception, remediated by the authenticity requirement; and the unmeasured-feeling claimant whose creative debates substitute executive gut response for audience affect measurement, remediated by the validation discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
