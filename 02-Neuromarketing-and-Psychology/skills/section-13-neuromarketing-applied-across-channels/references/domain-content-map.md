## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the channel-application discipline where the science of prior sections executes in the specific commercial surfaces consumers actually encounter: the packaging-application system (the shelf-distance hierarchy where package design faces recognition at approach distance, comprehension at arm distance, and evaluation at handling distance, each demanding different design elements per the packaging literatures; the sensory-package-integration where color blocks, shape silhouettes, material textures, opening mechanics, and sound of use combine into the multisensory product experience per the crossmodal sections; the package-testing methodology where eye tracking verifies hierarchy, haptic assessment verifies handling, and implicit tests verify association transfer per the research-practice section; the unboxing-experience design where reveal sequencing, layer structure, and included artifacts engineer the peak-moment and touch-ownership effects documented in the memory and haptic sections per the experience literatures); the retail-environment application (the store-navigation science where layout, signage, and sightlines guide movement and attention through documented wayfinding principles per the environmental literatures; the shelf-optics discipline where facing geometry, color blocking, vertical-horizontal organization, and eye-level salience determine visibility competition per the shopper literatures; the atmosphere-engineering system where lighting, music tempo, scent congruence, and temperature combine into the ambient affect documented in the sensory section per the atmospherics traditions; the checkout-moment design where queue psychology, impulse-zone architecture, and payment-interaction stress receive measurement per the decision-neuroscience connections); the digital-interface application (the landing-page-attention architecture where above-fold hierarchy, gaze-cue deployment, and form-design fluency apply the perception section to conversion surfaces with the eye-tracking verification loop; the app-engagement-design where onboarding flows, notification strategy, and interaction feedback implement the reward-loop and habit mechanics within the ethical bounds of the motivation section; the interface-fluency standards where loading response, transition smoothness, and interaction predictability carry the processing-fluency effects into measurable user experience per the UX literatures; the thumbnail-and-feed-optimization where scroll-context salience, size-adaptive hierarchy, and instant-recognition design serve the compressed-attention reality of mobile feeds per the feed literatures of the perception section); the video-and-broadcast application (the ad-film-testing practice where second-by-second physiological response tracking maps attention, arousal, and emotional valence onto narrative structure identifying weak frames and peak moments per the advertising-research literatures; the opening-seconds discipline where the first moments determine continued exposure through orienting-response capture per the attention literature, driving cold-open and hook-first structures; the audio-visual-synchronization craft where music, voice, cutting rhythm, and visual movement align per the crossmodal-congruence principles; the branding-moment-placement where brand integration timing faces the memory-encoding tradeoffs between early anchoring and narrative-flow interruption per the brand-placement literatures); the audio-and-radio application (the theater-of-mind principle where audio-only channels engage imagery generation whose elaboration depth differs from visual processing per the audio literatures; the voice-character-craft where timbre, pace, and accent carry the sound-symbolism and trust effects documented in the sensory section; the sonic-logo-integration where audio-identity assets deploy within spot structures per the sonic-branding traditions; the podcast-and-streaming-context application where host-read authenticity and content adjacency produce the parasocial trust effects per the influencer literatures of the companion course); the out-of-home and experiential application (the exposure-brevity design where transit and roadside formats face seconds of viewing demanding single-message clarity per the OOH literatures; the environmental-integration tactics where location context, scale contrast, and physical-dimension effects produce the novelty and shareability documented in the social-proof economics; the experiential-activation design where physical installations engineer multisensory immersion and social-content generation per the experience literatures; the digital-out-of-home dynamics where screen motion, daypart adaptation, and contextual relevance extend traditional OOH per the programmatic-OOH connections of the media-mathematics companion); and the cross-channel-integration practice (the sensory-identity-consistency discipline where brand assets, emotional register, and fluency standards persist across channels per the memory-section consistency principle; the channel-strength-matching where each surface deploys the modalities and mechanisms it physically supports rather than forcing uniform creative across incompatible contexts; the journey-measurement-integration where channel-specific studies connect into the journey-stage framework of the companion course per the orchestration traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the shelf-blind-packager whose beautiful designs lose recognition battles at actual shelf distance and lighting, remediated by the distance-hierarchy discipline; the atmosphere-incoherent retailer whose lighting fights the music fights the scent, fragmenting the ambient affect investment, remediated by the engineering-system integration; the desktop-designed-mobile optimizer whose landing hierarchies collapse under feed-scroll conditions never tested, remediated by the thumbnail-optimization practice; the last-five-seconds-brander whose film testing reveals brand recall failure from late-placement decisions made without measurement, remediated by the placement tradeoffs; the channel-force-fitter whose audio creative runs visual-dependent concepts on radio, remediated by the strength-matching discipline; and the study-without-iteration operator whose channel research produces reports while creative ships unchanged, remediated by the verification-loop integration with detection methods as the diagnostic.
