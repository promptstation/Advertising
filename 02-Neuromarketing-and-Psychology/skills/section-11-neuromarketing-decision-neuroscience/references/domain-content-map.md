## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the neuroeconomics layer where valuation, pricing, and choice mechanics of the companion behavioral course receive their biological grounding and measurement access: the valuation-system literacy (the common-currency-integration where heterogeneous product attributes, prices, and emotional reactions converge into unified subjective-value signals whose neural correlates track revealed preference and willingness to pay per the neuroeconomics traditions of the brain section; the attribute-integration dynamics where value computation weights attributes non-linearly with reference dependence, matching the prospect-theory patterns at the neural level per the neuroeconomic-prospect literatures; the state-modulation principle where hunger, mood, arousal, and time pressure shift valuation weighting, explaining context effects on price sensitivity and impulse per the state literatures); the price-processing neuroscience (the pain-of-paying-neural evidence where excessive price presentation engages aversion-associated processing including the documented insula response patterns per the price-neuroscience literatures; the payment-form-modulation where decoupled payment methods reduce aversive response signatures, the neural correlate of the cash-versus-card effects in the pricing section of the companion course; the discount-and-gain processing where promotional frames engage reward-anticipation circuitry with the reference-price-dependence of whether identical prices read as gain or loss per the promotion literatures; the price-quality-inference circuitry where premium pricing activates expectation and experience-modulation pathways including the documented wine-taste studies where price altered experienced pleasantness and its neural signature per the marketing-placebo literatures); the choice-process neuroscience (the option-comparison dynamics where choice among alternatives engages valuation comparison with documented response-time and conflict signatures including the compromise-effect correlates per the choice literatures; the choice-overload-evidence where large option sets produce decision-difficulty signatures and deferral, the neural backing of the assortment research in the companion course; the default-processing-advantage where default options receive reduced deliberative processing and implied-endorsement valuation per the default literatures; the self-control-conflict architecture where impulse and deliberation systems compete in intertemporal and indulgence choices, the substrate of commitment-device and friction design per the dual-system traditions); the willingness-to-pay measurement practice (the neural-WTP-estimation methods where valuation-region activity predicts price acceptance with documented but modest incremental validity over behavioral measures per the WTP literatures; the bid-behavioral-methods where auction-style and choice-based incentive-compatible procedures remain the practical standard for price research per the conjoint traditions connecting to the analytics companion; the price-testing-integration where neural and physiological measures supplement rather than replace the experimental price testing of the companion discipline per the triangulation standards); the neuromarketing-of-decision applications (the offer-structure-optimization where bundle, tier, and guarantee designs face response measurement at valuation and aversion levels per the applied literatures; the shelf-and-menu architecture testing where arrangement effects on choice receive attention and preference measurement per the retail literatures of the applied section; the checkout-flow assessment where friction and payment moments face stress and abandonment-signal measurement per the UX literatures; the promotion-response prediction where deal framing effects get tested before mass deployment per the testing traditions); and the interpretation-humility discipline (the reverse-inference-hazard where observing region activation and concluding specific mental state commits the documented logical error because regions serve multiple functions, the most common vendor-report flaw per the neuroimaging-critique literatures; the small-effect-realism where neural decision findings carry effect sizes whose commercial translation requires field validation per the evidence-grading habits; the individual-variation-management where valuation circuitry varies across people making population-average claims weak for individual prediction per the variation literatures; the behavior-anchor principle where neural measures earn trust through demonstrated correlation with actual choice and purchase data per the validation traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the reverse-inference-committer whose reports read insula activity as proof of price pain without the logical and replication support, remediated by the interpretation-humility discipline; the neural-WTP-overtruster whose scanner-based price predictions skip incentive-compatible behavioral validation, remediated by the behavior-anchor principle; the state-blind-pricer whose price tests run on sated, rested, unpressured samples and generalize to hungry, tired, rushed shoppers, remediated by the state-modulation literacy; the discount-circuit-chaser whose promotion design maximizes reward-anticipation activation while ignoring the reference-price erosion documented in the companion course, remediated by the integration discipline; the choice-overload-denier whose mega-assortment strategy treats decision-difficulty signatures as noise, remediated by the choice-process literacy; and the vendor-neuro-spectacle buyer whose decision studies pay for imaging glamour that conjoint methods answered cheaply, remediated by the triangulation standards with detection methods as the diagnostic.
