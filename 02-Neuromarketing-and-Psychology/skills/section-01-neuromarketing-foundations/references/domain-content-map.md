## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Establishes what neuromarketing actually claims, measures, and deserves, separating the legitimate discipline from the myth that surrounds it: the discipline definition (the consumer-neuroscience field studying how brains and bodies respond to marketing stimuli using physiological and neural measurement alongside behavior, distinct from the vendor-marketing meaning of neuromarketing as a service category per the field's academic-practice literatures; the core proposition that a large share of valuation, preference, and choice processing occurs outside verbal report and conscious awareness, making self-report research structurally incomplete rather than merely noisy per the dual-process traditions of the behavioral-economics companion; the measurement-complement principle where neural and physiological data add to, not replace, surveys, behavior logs, and sales outcomes whose combination produces decisions none supports alone); the founding evidence line (the Pepsi-Paradox study where blind-taste preference and branded preference diverged with different neural signatures, demonstrating that brand knowledge measurably alters consumption experience itself per the canonical fMRI literatures; the Zaltman imagery-elicitation lineage where metaphor-based methods preceded instrumentation in probing non-verbal response per the qualitative-neuroscience traditions; the affect-and-decision work including somatic-marker research where bodily response patterns guide decision before conscious deliberation per the Damasio traditions; the advertising-effectiveness evidence where emotional campaign performance data from effectiveness databanks established that felt response predicts commercial outcomes better than recalled message content per the IPA traditions); the subconscious claim audit (the consciousness-continuum reality where processing ranges from clearly pre-attentive features through supraliminal primes to fully deliberate evaluation, demanding that practitioners specify which layer any claim occupies; the verbal-report-limits documentation where introspective access to preference formation is genuinely restricted per the introspection literatures, justifying physiological measurement; the hype-correction discipline where popular claims of brain-button-finding and purchase-decoding exceed evidence, requiring the skeptical literacy this course builds; the replication-awareness where celebrated priming and neuro findings face the same open-science scrutiny as the behavioral-economics companion documents); the commercial landscape (the vendor ecosystem spanning full-service neurolabs, tool-based platforms, and freelance specialists whose quality variance demands the buying literacy of the research-practice section; the in-house capability trend where eye tracking, implicit testing, and biometric tools migrate into agency and brand research teams; the cost-capability tiers where measurement sophistication scales from free heatmap proxies through mid-range eye tracking to laboratory fMRI whose price rarely matches its decision value per the fitness-for-purpose discipline); and the practitioner's orientation (the question-first methodology where research questions select tools rather than tools selecting questions; the triangulation habit where neuro evidence joins behavioral, attitudinal, and sales data before conclusions form; the stimulus-design foundation where clean experiments require controlled stimulus preparation whose craft the applied sections develop) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the brain-scan-decorator whose strategy decks borrow fMRI imagery to authorize ordinary opinions, remediated by the measurement-complement principle; the mind-control-believer whose budget chases subliminal myths while ignoring measurable salience and fluency effects, remediated by the claim-audit discipline; the self-report-despairer who discards all survey and interview data because introspection is limited, losing the stated-preference information that complements physiology, remediated by the triangulation habit; the vendor-mystique-buyer whose unexamined black-box reports become unfalsifiable doctrine, remediated by the buying literacy ahead; and the tool-first-planner who commissions the most impressive available instrument for questions it cannot answer, remediated by the question-first methodology with detection methods as the diagnostic.
