## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the credibility discipline whose failure mode — the tone-deaf campaign — the course description names as the central professional risk cultural literacy prevents: the authenticity-concept analysis (the authenticity-as-social-construction principle where genuineness gets attributed by audiences through pattern recognition rather than measured objectively, making it a perception system with discoverable rules per the authenticity literatures; the authenticity-claim-types where origin authenticity, craft authenticity, idiosyncratic authenticity, and moral authenticity constitute distinct claim structures audiences evaluate differently per the typology literatures; the paradox-of-performed-authenticity where deliberate authenticity production faces detection dynamics whose exposure destroys the claim per the paradox literatures of the digital section; the audience-sophistication-evolution where persuasion knowledge and cultural fluency make audiences progressively better at detecting commercial performance per the sophistication literatures connecting to the behavioral companion's reactance traditions); the tone-deafness-mechanism analysis (the context-blindness-pattern where campaigns ignore current events, community circumstances, or historical moment producing the deafness the term names per the failure literatures; the voice-mismatch-pattern where brand expression exceeds earned standing — slang use, solidarity claims, subcultural adoption — without relationship history per the mismatch patterns of the language and subculture sections; the suffering-appropriation-pattern where social pain, movement symbolism, or tragedy association serves commercial ends without substance per the appropriation literatures; the research-failure-pattern where campaigns never faced the audience review that basic fieldwork would have provided per the methods traditions of the ethnography section); the cultural-appropriation-framework (the appropriation-versus-appreciation-analysis where taking without understanding, credit, reciprocity, or permission differs from engagement with relationship and respect per the framework literatures of the ethics section; the power-dimension-principle where appropriation harm concentrates when dominant commercial actors extract from marginalized communities per the power literatures; the case-pattern-library where documented industry failures — hairstyle marketing, indigenous imagery, religious symbol use, music-culture extraction — establish recognizable risk patterns per the case literatures; the legitimate-engagement-pathways where collaboration, compensation, credit, and community benefit structure ethical cultural exchange per the pathway traditions); the representation-discipline (the representation-quality-standards where casting, characterization, narrative role, creative-team composition, and cultural detail determine whether representation reads as genuine per the standards literatures of the identity section; the tokenism-detection-principles where symbolic inclusion without substance, power, or accuracy faces audience recognition per the tokenism literatures; the stereotype-mechanism-vigilance where representational harm operates through pattern, framing, and absence as much as explicit content per the mechanism literatures of the identity section; the inclusive-creative-process practice where diverse teams, community consultation, and sensitivity review build representation quality structurally per the process traditions); the credibility-protection practice (the pre-campaign-cultural-review where systematic review protocols catch deafness patterns before launch per the review traditions of the practice section; the community-advisor-engagement where relevant community voices participate in development with compensation and genuine influence per the engagement traditions; the response-protocol-preparation where missteps receive acknowledgment, correction, and learning rather than defensive minimization per the response literatures of the global section; the credibility-asset-management where earned authenticity accumulates through sustained consistent behavior and depletes through violation per the asset traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the authenticity-claimant-performer whose manufactured genuineness faces detection and produces the backlash that performed authenticity always risks, remediated by the paradox literacy; the calendar-blind-launcher whose campaign releases into unexamined current context, remediated by the context-blindness analysis; the solidarity-borrower whose movement symbolism fronts absent operational substance, remediated by the appropriation framework; the token-inclusion-defender whose representation quantity masks quality failures in role, voice, and process, remediated by the representation discipline; the review-skipper whose timeline pressure eliminates community consultation and cultural review, converting schedule savings into crisis cost, remediated by the protection practice; and the defensive-minimizer whose misstep response disputes audience perception instead of acknowledging harm, compounding the original failure, remediated by the response protocols with detection methods as the diagnostic.
