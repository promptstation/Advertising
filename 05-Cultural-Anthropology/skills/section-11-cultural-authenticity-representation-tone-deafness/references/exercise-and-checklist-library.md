# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Protect credibility through authenticity analysis (social-construction principle
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Cultural Anthropology scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** protect credibility through authenticity analysis (social-construction principle, claim types, performance paradox, audience sophistication), tone-deafness mechanisms (context blindness, voice mismatch, suffering appropriation, research failure), the appropriation framework (versus-appreciation analysis, power dimension, case patterns, legitimate pathways), representation discipline (quality standards, tokenism detection, stereotype vigilance, inclusive process), and credibility protection (pre-campaign review, advisor engagement, response protocols, asset management), avoiding performed authenticity, calendar blindness, solidarity borrowing, token defense.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.
