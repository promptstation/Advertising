# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Analyze identity structures through the category framework (social identity connection
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Cultural Anthropology scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** analyze identity structures through the category framework (social identity connection, intersectionality, constructed-lived distinction, fluidity awareness), gender systems (cultural-system understanding, representation history, contemporary dynamics, category sensitivity), generation discipline (cohort literacy, horoscope critique, life-stage distinction, intergenerational dynamics), class and taste (Bourdieu application, taste markers, aspirational dynamics, contemporary complexity), ethnicity and representation (meaning-system understanding, authenticity requirements, stereotype mechanisms, multicultural markets), and applied practice (identity mapping, representation review, community consultation, relevance judgment), avoiding single-category reduction, generation horoscopes, class blindness, token inclusion, checklist compliance, and irrelevance assumptions failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.
