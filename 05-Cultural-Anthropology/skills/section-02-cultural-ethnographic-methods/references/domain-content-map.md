# Domain Content Map — Ethnographic Methods: Fieldwork, Observation, and Thick Description

Read this during Phase 3 (architecture) and Phase 4 (writing) of the module
development workflow. Verify all factual claims and numbers against the
authoritative sources before publishing (Phase 2 evidence base); flag
anything disputed rather than repeating it.

## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Execute ethnographic research through fieldwork foundations (participant observation, immersion duration, positionality reflexivity, notes craft), observation toolkits (thick description, setting documentation, interaction observation, material analysis), interview methods (unstructured conversation, informant relationships, narrative methods, interview skill), consumer adaptations (in-home research, shopping observation, accompanied methods, diary studies), netnography (online-community method, digital field craft, multi-platform triangulation, data management), analysis discipline (coding, cross-case comparison, member checking, memos).
2. Design integration (question-method matching, mixed methods, commercial adaptation, documentation standards), avoiding armchair ethnography, survey interviews, notes neglect, projection interpretation, lurking-only netnography, and vibe reporting failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the methodological core whose immersive discipline produces the cultural understanding no survey, dataset, or feed-scroll delivers: the fieldwork foundation (the participant-observation method where sustained presence in the studied community combines involvement and observation, the discipline's defining practice per the Malinowski-lineage traditions of the foundations section; the immersion-duration principle where meaning systems reveal themselves over time because single-visit observation captures performance rather than practice per the fieldwork literatures; the positionality-reflexivity discipline where the researcher's identity, assumptions, and effect on the field get documented because neutral observation is a fiction per the reflexivity traditions; the field-notes craft where descriptive, interpretive, and reflexive notes accumulate as the primary data whose writing discipline determines analytic quality per the notes literatures); the observation-technique toolkit (the thick-description method where behavior gets recorded with its layered context of meaning, intention, and interpretation — the wink versus the blink distinction that defines ethnographic account per the Geertzian traditions of the sources; the setting-and-scene-documentation where physical spaces, temporal rhythms, and social arrangements receive systematic description per the environmental literatures; the interaction-and-ritual-observation where conversation patterns, greeting sequences, and repeated practices reveal social structure per the interaction literatures; the artifact-and-material-analysis where objects, clothing, tools, and spaces get read as materialized culture per the material-culture traditions); the interview-and-conversation methods (the unstructured-and-semi-structured conversation where open dialogue yields the emic categories that structured questions preclude per the interview literatures; the informant-relationship development where key community members become cultural teachers through sustained reciprocal relationship per the key-informant traditions; the life-history-and-narrative methods where personal stories carry cultural patterns in individual form per the narrative traditions; the interview-skill-discipline where question sequencing, silence tolerance, and follow-up craft determine depth per the method literatures); the consumer-ethnography adaptations (the in-home-and-in-context research where consumption practices get observed in natural settings revealing the gap between stated and actual behavior per the consumer-ethnography traditions; the shopping-and-usage observation where retail behavior, product use, and disposal practices get documented per the commercial-ethnography literatures; the accompanied-shopping-and-ride-along methods where mobile immersion captures journey practices per the mobile traditions; the diary-and self-documentation studies where participant-generated longitudinal records extend observation across time per the diary literatures); the netnography system (the online-community-ethnography method adapted by Kozinets where digital field sites including forums, Discord servers, subreddit cultures, comment sections, and group chats receive immersive study per the netnography traditions of the sources; the digital-field-craft where lurking-versus-participation decisions, platform-native behavior learning, and screenshot-and-archival documentation adapt fieldwork to online contexts per the netnography-method literatures; the multi-platform-triangulation where single-community findings face cross-platform verification because platform cultures differ per the digital literatures; the data-volume-management where abundant digital text and image data require systematic sampling and coding rather than impressionistic scrolling per the analytic traditions); the analysis-and-interpretation discipline (the coding-and-theme-development practice where field data gets systematically organized into patterns and categories per the qualitative-analysis traditions; the cross-case-comparison method where multiple field sites and informants build robust interpretation per the comparison traditions; the member-checking practice where interpretations return to community members for verification, the discipline that guards against researcher projection per the validation literatures; the analytic-memo-writing where interpretation develops through documented reasoning per the memo traditions); and the research-design integration (the question-to-method-matching where ethnography suits meaning, practice, and context questions while surveys and experiments suit prevalence and causality questions per the design literatures connecting to the analytics companion; the mixed-methods-architecture where ethnographic depth and quantitative breadth combine per the integration traditions; the commercial-research-adaptation where timeline, access, and budget constraints of agency practice shape fieldwork design without abandoning its standards per the applied traditions; the insight-documentation standards where ethnographic findings persist as evidence-linked accounts rather than vibe summaries per the reporting traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the armchair-ethnographer whose two hours of feed scrolling gets presented as fieldwork, missing the sustained-immersion requirement entirely, remediated by the duration principle; the survey-in-interview-clothing practitioner whose leading structured questions foreclose the emic categories unstructured conversation reveals, remediated by the interview discipline; the notes-neglecting observer whose rich field experience evaporates because documentation never happened, remediated by the notes craft; the projection-prone interpreter whose analysis confirms prior assumptions because member checking and cross-case comparison never ran, remediated by the validation discipline; the lurking-only-netnographer whose community understanding misses the participation that earns insider meaning, remediated by the digital-field craft; and the vibe-reporter whose ethnographic deliverables carry adjectives instead of evidence-linked thick description, remediated by the documentation standards with detection methods as the diagnostic.
