## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Operate cultural intelligence through audit methodology (scope definition, layer architecture, semiotic execution, comparative dimensions), trend systems (scanning architecture, signal triage, product standards, forecasting accountability), insight management (repository architecture, evidence linkage, lifecycle management, distribution), research programs (always-on balance, method orchestration, vendor networks, capability building), rigor governance (interpretation review.
2. Position awareness, evidence standards, ethics integration), and organizational integration (literacy embedding, influence tracking, value demonstration, curiosity cultivation), avoiding questionnaire audits, triage-free scanning, insight graveyards, episodic research, interpretation monoculture, and untracked influence failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the operational discipline where anthropological method becomes sustained organizational practice rather than episodic research projects: the cultural-audit methodology (the audit-scope-definition where brand, category, campaign, or portfolio audits specify objects, questions, and evidence standards per the audit traditions; the multi-layer-analysis-architecture where surface expression, practice and behavior, meaning and belief, and structural condition layers receive systematic examination per the layer traditions of the foundations section; the semiotic-audit-execution where visual, verbal, and material sign systems get decoded per the semiotics toolkit of the language section; the comparative-audit-dimension where category conventions, competitor positions, and historical evolution frame findings per the comparison traditions); the trend-intelligence-operating-system (the scanning-architecture-design where structured monitoring across communities, platforms, media, retail, and events runs on schedules with defined sources and signals per the scanning traditions of the diffusion section; the signal-triage-methodology where observations get filtered through driver analysis, network evidence, and lifecycle position before escalation per the triage disciplines of the diffusion section; the intelligence-product-standards where trend reports carry evidence, confidence assessment, implication analysis, and recommended action rather than observation lists per the product traditions; the forecasting-accountability-practice where trend predictions get documented and scored against outcomes building calibration per the accountability traditions of the analytics companion); the insight-management systems (the insight-repository-architecture where validated cultural knowledge persists searchable and referenced, preventing the rediscovery cycles of institutional amnesia per the repository traditions of the analytics companion; the evidence-linkage-standards where insights carry their ethnographic, analytic, and quantitative support per the linkage disciplines of the strategy section; the insight-lifecycle-management where knowledge faces refresh and retirement as cultures evolve per the lifecycle traditions; the cross-function-insight-distribution where strategic, creative, media, and product teams receive insight in usable form per the distribution traditions); the research-program design (the always-on-versus-project-balance where continuous listening and periodic deep studies combine per the balance literatures; the method-portfolio-orchestration where netnography, in-home ethnography, interviews, surveys, and data analysis deploy by question per the orchestration traditions connecting to the methods section and analytics companion; the vendor-and-specialist-network management where research partners, local experts, and community consultants receive selection and quality governance per the vendor traditions of the media companion; the capability-building-practice where internal cultural-literacy development raises organizational consumption quality per the capability traditions); the quality-and-rigor governance (the interpretation-review-discipline where cultural claims face challenge from alternative readings before publication per the review traditions of the methods section; the bias-and-position-awareness where researcher perspective, client interest, and commercial motivation get acknowledged in interpretation per the reflexivity traditions of the ethnography section; the evidence-sufficiency-standards where claim strength matches evidence depth per the sufficiency disciplines of the strategy section; the ethical-review-integration where research and application face the ethics section's protocols per the governance traditions); and the organizational-integration practice (the cultural-literacy-embedding where anthropological thinking enters briefs, reviews, and decision forums as standard practice per the embedding traditions; the decision-influence-tracking where cultural intelligence connects to actual strategy, creative, and investment decisions per the influence traditions; the value-demonstration-craft where cultural practice articulates contribution in business language without reducing insight to metrics per the demonstration traditions; the culture-of-curiosity-cultivation where organizational norms reward genuine cultural interest over stereotype convenience per the cultivation traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the audit-by-questionnaire practitioner whose cultural audits collect opinions instead of examining expression, practice, meaning, and structure, remediated by the layer architecture; the scan-without-triage hoarder whose monitoring accumulates observations that never face driver analysis or lifecycle assessment, remediated by the triage methodology; the insight-graveyard keeper whose repositories hold brilliant analysis nobody can find, cite, or apply, remediated by the distribution practice; the episodic-research-organization whose project-between blindness misses the continuous cultural motion trends require, remediated by the always-on balance; the interpretation-monoculture shop whose single analyst reading faces no challenge review, remediated by the review discipline; and the untracked-influence function whose cultural intelligence never connects to decisions, justifying its elimination at first budget review, remediated by the integration practice with detection methods as the diagnostic.
