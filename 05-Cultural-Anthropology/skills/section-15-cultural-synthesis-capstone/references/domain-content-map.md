## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates the complete discipline from anthropological foundations through ethnographic method, subculture dynamics, language and semiotics, diffusion, ritual and myth, identity structures, global culture, digital life, strategic conversion, authenticity discipline, operating systems, ethics, and applied execution into the unified cultural capability the course description frames as preventing tone-deaf campaigns and enabling authentic speech to highly specific demographics: the discipline synthesis — the complete culture-to-campaign chain (the end-to-end architecture where anthropological lens frames the question, ethnographic method gathers evidence, subculture and identity analysis locates communities, language and semiotics decode expression, diffusion analysis positions timing, ritual and myth supply depth, global literacy manages translation, digital fluency navigates platforms, strategic conversion produces positioning, authenticity discipline protects credibility, operating systems sustain practice, ethics governs throughout, and applied craft executes, every section operating as one system; the evidence-meaning-craft triad where fieldwork rigor, interpretive depth, and commercial execution combine as the distinctive competency this course certifies, each insufficient alone — evidence without meaning produces data dumps, meaning without evidence produces projection, both without craft produces unused insight; the emic-primacy-foundation where insider meaning systems govern all judgment and outsider analysis serves rather than replaces them, the orientation that prevents tone-deafness structurally per the foundations section); the literacy-command integration (the methodological command where ethnography, netnography, and mixed methods produce evidence to professional standards per the methods section; the cultural-systems command where subcultures, language, ritual, identity, and digital life get analyzed through disciplinary frameworks per the middle sections; the diffusion-and-timing command where trend mechanics and lifecycle position inform strategic timing per the diffusion section; the strategic-conversion command where insight becomes positioning, briefs, community programs, and campaigns per the strategy and application sections); the diagnostic mastery — reading cultures, campaigns, and claims (the culture-audit protocol where any community or market faces expression, practice, meaning, and structure analysis through the audit systems of the practice section; the campaign-audit-protocol where any creative work faces context, voice, representation, appropriation, and timing interrogation synthesizing the authenticity and ethics failure libraries into the diagnostic reference; the trend-claim-audit protocol where any trend assertion faces driver analysis, lifecycle position, triangulation, and prediction-accountability review per the diffusion disciplines; the insight-claim-audit protocol where any cultural insight faces evidence-sufficiency, positionality, and alternative-reading challenge per the governance traditions); the capstone project — demonstrating integrated mastery (the capstone specification requiring candidates to complete a full cultural-strategy engagement for a real brand and market: an ethnographic research program with documented method, fieldwork or netnography of appropriate depth, and reflexive positionality statement; a cultural analysis covering subculture and identity structures, language and semiotic codes, ritual and mythic layers, and diffusion position for the target community; a strategic conversion producing tension territory, cultural positioning, and creative brief with evidence linkage; an applied program designing campaign, community, or co-creation execution with cultural-moment and ecosystem planning; a completed authenticity and ethics review covering appropriation risk, representation standards, community benefit, and consent questions; and a defense document anticipating cultural, strategic, and ethical challenges from community and client perspectives; the evidence-authenticity requirement engaging real communities through genuine research rather than stereotype assembly or feed impression; the evaluation criteria covering methodological rigor, interpretive depth, strategic coherence, ethical discipline, and execution craft; the portfolio development where capstone artifacts demonstrate professional capability); the professional-identity synthesis (the cultural-strategist identity where anthropological literacy, fieldwork skill, interpretive craft, ethical commitment, and commercial translation combine into the professional who makes brands culturally fluent rather than culturally loud; the humility-authority paradox mastery where deep confidence in method coexists with genuine openness to community correction, the productive tension defining mature practice; the community-respect orientation where the people studied function as ends rather than extraction fuel per the ethics section; the continuous-immersion commitment where cultural currency requires sustained participation in the communities and platforms analyzed rather than periodic study per the learning traditions); and the forward path (the discipline-frontier awareness covering AI-generated culture and synthetic media's effect on authenticity detection, platform-ecosystem evolution, post-cookie cultural-data constraints, decolonizing-method debates, and regulation of cultural targeting whose trajectories reshape practice; the specialization routes toward brand strategy, community management, trend intelligence, research leadership, or creative cultural direction building on this foundation; the companion-course connections where behavioral economics supplies decision science, neuromarketing supplies response measurement, predictive analytics supplies quantitative breadth, media mathematics supplies distribution machinery, and creative craft supplies expression per the curriculum family; the capability-development planning against this course's fifteen-section skill inventory with deliberate practice on identified gaps) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the framework-collector whose disciplinary vocabulary never converted into fieldwork, analysis, or campaign craft, remediated by the capstone's full-chain requirement; the insight-without-evidence projector whose cultural claims rest on personal reading rather than methodical research, remediated by the methodological command; the evidence-without-strategy researcher whose rigorous studies never reach briefs, positions, or programs, remediated by the conversion command; the ethics-as-constraint professional whose compliance-minimum posture misses that ethical engagement produces the trust and access that better research and campaigns require, remediated by the identity synthesis; the capstone-as-armchair exercise whose community analysis never engaged actual community members, remediated by the evidence-authenticity requirement; and the destination-illusion treating course completion as cultural fluency arrival rather than the start of immersion-by-immersion, community-by-community learning, remediated by the continuous-immersion commitment with detection methods as the diagnostic.

## Unit 2 — Capstone integration

**Purpose:** integrate every capability above on one realistic problem end to end.

**Structure:** the learner takes a single problem in Cultural Anthropology through each unit's framework in sequence, producing a coherent deliverable that mirrors real professional documentation. Evaluation criteria: internal consistency across artifacts, evidence discipline, honest trade-off statements, and demonstrable use of each unit's framework.
