## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Practice cultural ethics through research foundations (consent systems, harm principles, anonymity standards, reciprocity), commercial adaptation (code integration, dual-purpose transparency, data governance, vulnerable protections).
2. Use ethics (extraction-exchange distinction, benefit design, cultural-use consent, harm anticipation), representation practice (voice and authority, accuracy obligation, power management, credit attribution), professional conduct (integrity standards, client counsel, industry norms, personal reflexivity), and operations integration (review workflow, red-flag recognition, documentation, repair learning), avoiding consent fiction, extraction normalization, voice usurpation, client-order following, reflexivity scoffing, and gate opposition failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the ethical discipline whose anthropological foundations — built from the field's colonial-history reckoning — carry special weight when commercial interests fund cultural study: the research-ethics foundation (the informed-consent-system where research participants understand and agree to study conditions with the digital-context complications of public posts, community observation, and platform data per the consent literatures of the netnography traditions; the do-no-harm-principle where research activity avoids community disruption, exposure, and reprisal per the disciplinary codes of anthropology; the anonymity-and-confidentiality-standards where participant and community identification gets protected with the small-community recognizability challenges documented per the protection literatures; the reciprocity-expectation where studied communities receive value — insight sharing, compensation, support — rather than pure extraction per the reciprocity traditions); the commercial-research-ethics adaptation (the market-research-code-integration where association codes covering honesty, privacy, and participant treatment apply to ethnographic practice per the code literatures; the dual-purpose-transparency where community members understand commercial research intent rather than believing participation serves academic or neutral ends per the transparency disciplines; the data-ownership-and-use-governance where cultural knowledge collected from communities faces use limitations and retention standards per the governance traditions of the analytics companion; the vulnerable-population-protections where children, marginalized communities, and crisis-affected populations receive heightened consent and harm-avoidance standards per the protection literatures of the identity section); the cultural-use-ethics framework (the extraction-versus-exchange distinction where taking cultural knowledge and expression for commercial gain differs from exchange with relationship, credit, and benefit per the appropriation frameworks of the authenticity section; the community-benefit-design where cultural engagement structures compensation, platforming, sourcing, and investment back into originating communities per the benefit traditions; the consent-for-cultural-use question where community permission for representation and code use gets sought through legitimate representatives with the who-speaks-for-community problem acknowledged per the consent literatures; the harm-anticipation-discipline where cultural deployment faces assessment of stereotype reinforcement, community exposure, backlash risk, and misuse potential per the anticipation traditions); the representation-ethics practice (the voice-and-authority-questions where who tells whose story with what standing structures representation ethics per the voice literatures of the representation traditions; the accuracy-obligation where cultural portrayal faces community-verification standards per the accuracy traditions of the authenticity section; the power-asymmetry-management where brand resources and platform dwarf community capacity, requiring deliberate fairness in collaboration terms per the asymmetry literatures; the credit-and-attribution-practice where cultural sources receive acknowledgment per the attribution traditions); the professional-conduct system (the practitioner-integrity-standards where personal honesty about method, evidence, and limitation governs professional work per the integrity traditions; the client-ethics-counsel role where cultural professionals push back on appropriative, stereotyping, and exploitative briefs with the escalation pathways per the counsel traditions; the industry-norm-development where professional associations, case-study sharing, and public accountability raise field standards per the norm traditions; the personal-position-reflexivity where practitioners examine their own cultural position, privilege, and blind spots as standing discipline per the reflexivity traditions of the ethnography section); and the ethics-operations integration (the ethics-review-workflow where research protocols and campaign applications face structured review per the review traditions of the practice section; the red-flag-pattern-recognition where extractive language, timeline pressure on consultation, and stereotype convenience signal ethical drift per the recognition traditions; the documentation-and-accountability systems where ethical decisions and their rationale persist per the accountability traditions; the repair-and-learning-practice where ethical failures receive acknowledgment, correction, and systemic learning per the repair traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the consent-fiction-collector whose public-post scraping gets labeled observation while community members never knew they were studied, remediated by the digital-consent literacies; the extraction-normalizer whose commercial take-from-community practice faces no benefit design because exchange was never considered, remediated by the framework discipline; the voice-usurper whose brand tells community stories that community members were available and willing to tell, remediated by the authority questions; the client-order-follower whose professional judgment defers to appropriative briefs because pushback costs comfort, remediated by the counsel role; the reflexivity-scoffer whose practitioner examines every culture but their own position, importing blind spots as objectivity, remediated by the position discipline; and the ethics-as-gate-opposer who treats review as obstacle rather than quality system until public failure reclassifies it, remediated by the operations integration with detection methods as the diagnostic.
