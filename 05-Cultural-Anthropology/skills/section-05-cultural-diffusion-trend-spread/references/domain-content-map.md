## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the diffusion discipline whose spread mechanics the course description names as core study, converting trend observation from vibes into analyzed process: the diffusion-theory foundation (the Rogers-adoption-curve framework where innovations spread through innovators, early adopters, early majority, late majority, and laggards whose characteristics and network positions determine diffusion pace per the diffusion literatures of the sources; the opinion-leadership-and-two-step-flow traditions where influence concentrates in socially connected interpreters whose endorsement carries adoption weight per the flow literatures; the adoption-decision-process model where knowledge, persuasion, decision, implementation, and confirmation stages structure individual uptake per the process traditions; the innovation-attribute-effects where relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, and observability predict diffusion speed per the attribute literatures); the cultural-transmission mechanics (the meme-as-unit-of-transmission debate where Dawkins-lineage cultural-replication concepts inform but oversimplify the reconstruction and transformation that actual cultural spread involves per the memetics literatures; the transformation-in-diffusion principle where practices, styles, and meanings mutate as they travel between communities, making spread a translation process rather than copy process per the transmission traditions; the network-structure-effects where dense-cluster cohesion and weak-tie bridges jointly determine diffusion reach per the network literatures; the platform-algorithm-mediation where recommendation systems now accelerate, select, and distort cultural spread, making algorithmic logic a diffusion variable per the algorithm literatures connecting to the media companion course); the trend-lifecycle analysis (the emergence-phase-dynamics where trends originate in subcultural practice, fringe communities, or structural conditions whose identification the ethnographic methods support per the emergence literatures; the acceleration-phase-markers where early-adopter uptake, media coverage, and commercial attention compound visibility per the acceleration traditions; the peak-and-saturation-detection where mainstream adoption signals both maximum reach and originating-community abandonment, the double-edge that punishes late commercial entry per the saturation literatures of the subculture section; the decline-and-transformation-patterns where trends die, persist as convention, or mutate into successor forms per the lifecycle traditions); the cool-hunting-and-trend-intelligence practice (the systematic-scanning-methodology where structured monitoring across communities, platforms, markets, and categories replaces impressionistic trend spotting per the scanning traditions of the practice section; the weak-signal-interpretation discipline where fringe observations get assessed for structural driver, network position, and replication evidence before promotion to trend claim per the signal literatures connecting to the analytics companion's trend section; the triangulation-verification practice where candidate trends face cross-source, cross-platform, and cross-community confirmation per the verification traditions; the prediction-versus-documentation honesty where trend intelligence distinguishes genuine anticipation from post-hoc narrative, the calibration discipline per the forecasting literatures of the analytics companion); the commercial-diffusion-application (the diffusion-position-strategy where brand entry timing gets chosen against trend lifecycle position with the early-risk versus late-irrelevance tradeoff per the timing literatures; the bridge-audience-identification where connector communities between origin and mainstream receive strategic attention per the bridge traditions; the diffusion-acceleration-tactics where trialability design, observability engineering, and opinion-leader partnership speed legitimate adoption per the tactic literatures within the ethics boundaries; the counter-diffusion-awareness where negative information, backlash movements, and boycott dynamics spread by the same mechanics per the counter literatures); and the structural-driver analysis (the condition-based-trend-reading where trends get traced to economic pressure, technological enablement, demographic shift, regulatory change, and generational transition rather than treated as spontaneous per the driver literatures of the analytics companion; the cultural-contradiction-mapping where Holt's framework identifies societal tensions that iconic brands and cultural movements address per the contradiction literatures of the sources; the pattern-recognition-versus-pattern-projection discipline where historical diffusion cases inform without determining because structural conditions shift per the pattern traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the curve-misreader whose adoption-stage labels get applied to audiences without network or attribute analysis, producing demographic astrology, remediated by the framework literacy; the peak-arrival-merchant whose trend campaigns launch at saturation while originating communities exit, remediated by the lifecycle detection; the weak-signal-amplifier whose single viral post gets promoted to trend without driver analysis or triangulation, remediated by the interpretation discipline; the documentation-as-prediction spinner whose already-mainstream observations get narrated as foresight, destroying forecasting accountability, remediated by the honesty discipline; the algorithm-blind-diffusionist whose spread analysis ignores platform mediation that selects and distorts what travels, remediated by the mediation literacy; and the copy-diffusion-assumer whose strategy expects trends to transfer unchanged across communities and markets despite the transformation principle, remediated by the transmission mechanics with detection methods as the diagnostic.
