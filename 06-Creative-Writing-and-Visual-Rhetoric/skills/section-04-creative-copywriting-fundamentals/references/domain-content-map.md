## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the working-copy discipline whose component craft — headline, body, and call to action — constitutes the daily production of persuasive commercial writing: the headline-system depth (the headline-function-taxonomy where selection, attention, news delivery, and audience-qualification functions determine construction per the Schwartz traditions of the sources; the headline-form-inventory where benefit statements, mechanism revelations, questions, commands, testimonials, and price-led openings suit different awareness and sophistication stages per the form literatures; the headline-testing-heritage where the direct-response tradition's split-test discipline established empirical headline evaluation per the testing literatures connecting to the performance section; the subhead-and-deck-craft where secondary lines extend, qualify, and bridge headline promises into body per the bridge traditions); the body-copy architecture (the classic-formula-systems where attention-interest-desire-action, problem-agitate-solve, and before-after-bridge structures organize persuasion sequences whose formula fluency enables rather than replaces thinking per the formula literatures; the Schwartz-desire-channeling-principle where copy directs existing market desire toward the product rather than creating desire from nothing, the foundational doctrine per the Breakthrough traditions of the sources; the lead-paragraph-craft where body openings honor headline promises and establish the reading contract per the lead literatures; the flow-and-transition-discipline where sentence rhythm, paragraph length variation, and connective logic maintain reading momentum per the flow traditions of the hook section; the proof-integration-systems where claims meet evidence through demonstration, statistics, testimonials, authority citation, and specificity per the proof literatures connecting to the social-proof mechanics of the behavioral companion; the objection-handling-architecture where anticipated resistance receives acknowledgment and resolution within body flow per the objection traditions); the call-to-action craft (the CTA-clarity-principle where action instructions specify exactly what to do with the friction-removal language per the clarity literatures; the value-restatement-discipline where action moments restate benefit rather than command bare clicks per the restatement traditions; the urgency-and-scarcity-integration where legitimate time and quantity constraints connect to the behavioral companion's scarcity systems within the honesty boundaries the ethics section enforces per the integration literatures; the CTA-placement-and-repetition-judgment where action opportunities distribute through copy length per the placement traditions); the format-length discipline (the long-copy-doctrine where the direct-response evidence supports length proportional to claim complexity and price stakes, with the qualified-audience reality that readers self-select per the length literatures; the short-copy-craft where compressed persuasion runs on single dominant idea and immediate action per the compression traditions; the body-copy-readability-standards where grade-level simplicity serves processing fluency without condescension per the readability literatures connecting to the fluency systems of the neuromarketing companion; the skimming-architecture where formatting elements serve the majority scanning behavior per the scan traditions); the product-knowledge foundation (the feature-benefit-transformation discipline where technical attributes convert into experienced value per the transformation literatures of the Ogilvy traditions; the demonstration-craft where product performance gets shown through vivid scenario rather than claimed through adjective per the demonstration traditions; the research-immersion-requirement where copywriters study product, customer, market, and competition to the depth the Ogilvy doctrine demands per the immersion literatures); and the copy-evaluation practice (the message-market-fit-assessment where copy faces the awareness-stage and sophistication-stage alignment checks of the foundations section; the single-mindedness-test where each piece carries one dominant proposition per the discipline traditions; the read-aloud-and-clarity-audit where rhythm and comprehension face the craft standards of the foundations section; the conversion-logic-review where the path from hook through proof to action holds without gap per the logic traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the formula-painter whose AIDA templates receive surface fill without desire research or proof substance, remediated by the channeling principle; the claim-without-evidence-writer whose adjective-stacked assertions meet sophisticated-audience skepticism, remediated by the proof systems; the buried-CTA-composer whose copy persuades completely then fails to request action clearly, remediated by the clarity principle; the length-dogmatist whose short-copy religion truncates complex high-stakes arguments or whose long-copy habit exhausts simple low-stakes ones, remediated by the length doctrine; the feature-list-reciter whose specification catalogs never transform into experienced benefit, remediated by the transformation discipline; and the single-draft-shipper whose copy faces no evaluation audit because evaluation practice never became habit, remediated by the evaluation practice with detection methods as the diagnostic.
