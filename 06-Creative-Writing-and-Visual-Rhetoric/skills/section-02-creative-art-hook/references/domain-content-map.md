## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the attention-capture discipline whose three-second window the course description names as the defining constraint of modern persuasive writing: the attention-economy foundation (the scroll-velocity-reality where feed environments allocate fractions of seconds per message, making the opening functionally the entire audition per the attention literatures of the media companion course; the orienting-response-mechanics where novelty, movement, contrast, and emotional charge trigger automatic attention per the perception systems of the neuromarketing companion; the pattern-interruption-principle where expectation violation breaks automatic filtering with the comprehension-boundary that unintelligible interruption merely annoys per the pattern literatures; the relevance-gate reality where captured attention survives only milliseconds longer unless self-interest recognition follows, making hook and promise inseparable per the gate literatures); the headline-and-opening-line systems (the Schwartz-headline-doctrine where headlines select audience and deliver benefit or mechanism promise to the awareness stage per the Breakthrough Advertising traditions of the sources; the Ogilvy-headline-discipline where readership research established that headline carries the majority of attention investment per the Ogilvy traditions of the sources; the opening-line-craft where first sentences create the gap, question, or tension that demands continuation per the opening literatures; the four-U and similar evaluation frameworks where useful, urgent, unique, and ultra-specific criteria systematize headline quality per the evaluation traditions); the hook-type catalog (the benefit-promise-hook where direct value proposition suits aware audiences and performance contexts per the promise literatures; the curiosity-gap-hook where incomplete information creates the itch that only continuation scratches, with the clickbait-degradation boundary documented per the gap literatures; the pattern-break-hook where formal or content unexpectedness stops automatic processing per the interruption mechanics; the story-in-motion-hook where narrative already underway creates immediate investment per the narrative literatures of the storytelling section; the question-and-challenge-hooks where direct address and provocative position invite engagement with the reactance-boundary awareness per the challenge literatures; the social-proof-and-authority-hooks where credibility signals front-load trust per the proof connections of the behavioral companion; the visual-hook-integration where image, motion, and typography carry hook function jointly with words per the visual-rhetoric section); the medium-specific-hook practice (the short-video-hook-architecture where first-frame visual, opening line, and pacing structure retention curves whose early drop-off the analytics reveal per the video literatures of the format section; the social-feed-hook-conventions where platform-native opening patterns differ across feed cultures per the platform literatures of the anthropology companion; the search-and-headline-hooks where query-intent alignment determines click capture per the search traditions of the media companion; the email-subject-and-preheader-systems where inbox competition runs hook mechanics at scale per the email literatures of the format section; the landing-page-above-fold-discipline where arriving attention faces immediate promise confirmation per the post-click traditions of the media companion); the hook-body-continuity discipline (the promise-delivery-contract where hooks create obligations the body must honor because violation produces the bounce and distrust the substance pitfall documents per the continuity literatures; the open-loop-architecture where partial resolutions sequence attention through body content per the loop traditions; the escalation-craft where each paragraph ending pulls the next reading per the flow literatures; the payoff-design discipline where hook-promised value delivers at documented points per the payoff traditions); and the hook-development practice (the swipe-file-and-analysis discipline where professional collections of effective hooks get reverse-engineered into mechanism understanding per the analysis traditions of the process section; the volume-and-variation-methodology where hook development produces many candidates because response prediction remains uncertain per the testing traditions of the performance section; the audience-calibration-judgment where hook intensity and style match awareness stage, platform culture, and brand voice per the calibration literatures) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the slow-start-burier whose value proposition hides behind throat-clearing sentences that scroll environments never permit, remediated by the attention-economy literacy; the clickbait-degrader whose curiosity gaps deliver no substance, buying clicks that bounce and training audience distrust, remediated by the continuity discipline; the universal-hook-applier whose single hook style runs across platforms, audiences, and awareness stages, remediated by the calibration judgment; the clever-hook-obscurer whose wordplay entertains while the actual promise stays unclear, remediated by the relevance-gate literacy; the single-draft-settler whose first hook attempt ships because volume methodology never operated, remediated by the development practice; and the visual-verbal-splitter whose image and headline argue different messages, dividing the three-second budget, remediated by the integration discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
