## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the format-specific discipline where universal craft adapts to the physical, temporal, and cultural constraints of each medium: the format-thinking foundation (the medium-message-fit principle where format constraints — duration, dimensions, sound environment, attention context — shape what persuasion structures can operate per the format literatures; the native-convention-discipline where each medium carries audience-learned conventions whose fluent use outperforms imported structures per the convention literatures connecting to the platform cultures of the anthropology companion; the format-portfolio-awareness where campaigns distribute ideas across formats whose strengths differ per the portfolio traditions of the media companion's planning section; the constraint-as-creative-resource reframe where limits including character counts, silent autoplay, and six-second durations generate inventive compression per the constraint literatures); the social-format craft (the feed-copy-systems where platform-native opening patterns, caption architecture, and comment-section engagement shape social writing per the feed literatures of the hook section; the short-video-script-craft where hook-first structure, pacing beats, on-screen text layers, and sound-off legibility organize scripts for vertical formats per the video literatures; the carousel-and-sequence-forms where multi-frame storytelling distributes argument across slides per the sequence traditions of the rhetoric section; the meme-and-trend-participation-craft where format-native humor and trend response operate within the authenticity boundaries of the anthropology companion per the participation literatures; the platform-character-limits discipline where compression to constraint preserves message core per the compression traditions); the video-and-broadcast craft (the commercial-script-architecture where scene structure, visual-audio interplay, and timing discipline organize broadcast and streaming spots per the script literatures; the narrative-arc-compression where story structures of the storytelling section compress into fifteen, thirty, and sixty-second forms per the compression traditions; the dialogue-and-voiceover-craft where spoken language carries rhythm and clarity requirements distinct from written copy per the spoken literatures connecting to the read-aloud standards of the foundations; the visual-storyboarding-collaboration where scripts develop with visual plan per the board traditions of the collaboration section; the branded-content-and-documentary-forms where long-form video carries sustained narrative with disclosure requirements per the content literatures of the ethics section); the audio-and-radio craft (the theater-of-mind-writing where sound-only formats engage listener imagery through voice, sound effect, and music cues per the audio literatures connecting to the multisensory findings of the neuromarketing companion; the radio-and-streaming-spot-structure where timing precision and single-message discipline govern the form per the spot traditions; the podcast-advertisement-craft where host-read and produced segments carry the parasocial trust dynamics per the podcast literatures of the media companion; the sonic-identity-integration where audio logos and sonic palettes deploy per the sonic traditions of the neuromarketing companion); the print-and-physical craft (the print-ad-architecture where headline, visual, body, and signature elements organize on the page per the print traditions of the Ogilvy doctrines; the packaging-copy-craft where panel hierarchy, regulatory text, and shelf-distance communication govern physical product writing per the packaging literatures connecting to the neuromarketing companion's package analysis; the direct-mail-and-physical-forms where tactile formats carry response mechanics per the mail traditions; the editorial-and-publication-writing where advertorial and sponsored content operate within disclosure requirements per the editorial literatures of the ethics section); the email-and-lifecycle craft (the subject-preheader-system where inbox competition runs the hook mechanics at personal scale per the email literatures of the hook section; the email-body-architecture where preview text, scan structure, and single-CTA discipline organize conversion messages per the body traditions; the lifecycle-sequence-design where welcome, nurture, abandonment, and win-back flows carry behavioral strategy into copy per the sequence literatures connecting to the journey frameworks of the behavioral companion; the deliverability-and-compliance-basics where sender reputation and regulation constrain practice per the compliance literatures of the ethics section); the out-of-home and environmental craft (the OOH-compression-discipline where seconds of exposure and distance viewing demand single-idea clarity per the out-of-home literatures of the media companion; the environmental-context-integration where location, movement, and surroundings become creative material per the context traditions; the digital-OOH-dynamics where motion, daypart variation, and data-triggered content extend the format per the digital traditions of the media companion); and the cross-format-adaptation practice (the idea-extension-methodology where campaign concepts translate across formats through essence preservation rather than mechanical resizing per the extension traditions; the format-testing-integration where format-specific execution faces the measurement systems of the performance section per the testing traditions; the production-efficiency-judgment where asset systems and modular production serve multi-format delivery per the production traditions of the art-direction section) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the format-agnostic-resizer whose single creative gets mechanically scaled across media, violating every native convention, remediated by the medium-fit principle; the sound-dependent-video-maker whose scripts assume audio that feed autoplay denies, remediated by the sound-off discipline; the character-limit-fighter whose copy wars with platform constraints instead of mastering compression, remediated by the compression craft; the theater-of-mind-illiterate whose audio scripts read as printed copy missing the imagery machinery sound provides, remediated by the audio craft; the OOH-billboard-stuffer whose six-second-exposure formats carry paragraph content, remediated by the compression discipline; and the essence-losing-adapter whose cross-format translation preserves assets while losing the idea, remediated by the extension methodology with detection methods as the diagnostic.
