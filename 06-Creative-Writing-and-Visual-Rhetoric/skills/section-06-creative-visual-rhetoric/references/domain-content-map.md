## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the visual-argument discipline where images persuade through rhetorical structure rather than decoration, the fastest-processing channel of commercial communication: the visual-rhetoric foundation (the images-as-argument principle where composition, symbol, and sequence make claims, present evidence, and invite inference with the same rhetorical functions as verbal argument per the visual-rhetoric literatures; the visual-grammar literacy where McCloud's analysis of line, shape, closure, and sequence establishes how images generate meaning through reader completion per the Understanding Comics traditions of the sources; the processing-advantage documentation where visual information processes faster and retains longer than verbal, making visual argument the primary persuasion channel in attention-scarce environments per the processing literatures of the neuromarketing companion; the verbal-visual-relationship taxonomy where images anchor text, text anchors images, or the two operate in counterpoint, the interaction whose design determines message coherence per the relationship literatures); the visual-figure toolkit (the visual-metaphor-system where concrete imagery carries abstract claims — speed through blur, freshness through dew, power through scale — with the comprehension-boundary discipline per the metaphor literatures; the visual-metonymy-and-synecdoche patterns where part stands for whole and association stands for subject, the compression mechanisms of efficient imagery per the figure traditions; the visual-hyperbole-and-irony forms where exaggeration and contrast carry humor and critique with the misreading-risk management per the irony literatures of the digital-culture section; the visual-antithesis-craft where before-after, with-without, and contrast compositions structure comparison arguments per the antithesis traditions connecting to the contrast principles of the behavioral companion); the composition-rhetoric practice (the visual-hierarchy-argument where size, position, contrast, and isolation assert importance claims per the hierarchy systems of the perception science; the gaze-and-pathway-design where viewer attention routes through composition carrying the argument sequence per the pathway literatures connecting to the eye-tracking methods of the neuromarketing companion; the framing-and-cropping-rhetoric where inclusion, exclusion, and proximity make claims about relationship and significance per the framing traditions; the negative-space-discipline where emptiness carries premium, focus, and breathing-room meaning per the space literatures of the design section); the image-content-rhetoric (the human-presence-effects where faces, gaze direction, expression, and body language carry social argument per the face literatures connecting to the gaze-cue findings of the neuromarketing companion; the product-presentation-rhetoric where hero shots, in-use context, and detail macros make different claims about value per the presentation traditions; the setting-and-context-argument where environments position products within lifestyle, status, and use-case claims per the context literatures connecting to the identity analysis of the anthropology companion; the color-and-light-rhetoric where palette and illumination carry emotional and quality argument per the color systems of the neuromarketing companion and art-direction section); the sequence-and-motion rhetoric (the narrative-sequence-design where image series carry story argument through the closure mechanisms of the comics traditions per the sequence literatures; the motion-and-editing-rhetoric where pacing, transition, and movement make claims about energy and relationship in video per the motion literatures of the format section; the interactive-visual-argument where user-controlled exploration carries discovery rhetoric per the interactive traditions of the storytelling section); the visual-claim-discipline (the demonstration-versus-decoration test where every visual element either argues or gets cut per the economy disciplines of the copy section; the claim-support-verification where visual assertions face the substantiation standards the ethics section enforces because images make claims too per the verification literatures; the cultural-visual-literacy where symbol, gesture, and color meanings travel across cultures per the audit traditions of the anthropology companion; the accessibility-visual-standards where contrast, alt-text, and non-visual equivalents serve inclusive communication per the accessibility traditions of the neuromarketing companion) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the decoration-designer whose beautiful imagery carries no argument, consuming production budget while copy does all persuasive work, remediated by the demonstration test; the metaphor-overextender whose visual figures require decoding effort that scroll environments never grant, remediated by the comprehension boundary; the verbal-visual-contradictor whose image and headline argue different claims, splitting the persuasion budget, remediated by the relationship taxonomy; the hierarchy-anarchist whose compositions present everything at equal emphasis, arguing nothing, remediated by the composition practice; the unsubstantiated-visual-claimer whose before-after imagery and demonstration scenes assert results the evidence cannot support, remediated by the verification discipline; and the culture-blind-visualist whose gestures, symbols, and settings travel into markets where meanings invert, remediated by the cultural-visual literacy with detection methods as the diagnostic.
