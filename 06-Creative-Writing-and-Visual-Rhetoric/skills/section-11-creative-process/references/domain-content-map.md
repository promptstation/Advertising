## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the process discipline whose systematic methods convert research and strategy into original creative work, demystifying inspiration as manageable craft: the process-architecture foundation (the double-diamond-model literacy where problem divergence and convergence precede solution divergence and convergence, structuring creative work per the process literatures; the preparation-immersion-stage where research absorption, product study, and audience understanding load the raw material that ideation recombines per the immersion traditions of the Ogilvy doctrines; the incubation-reality where unconscious processing requires deliberate scheduling of distance between immersion and ideation per the incubation literatures; the stage-discipline-principle where skipped preparation produces cliche and premature convergence produces mediocrity, the failure modes process structure prevents per the discipline traditions); the ideation-methodology toolkit (the divergent-thinking-practice where quantity-before-quality generation with judgment suspension produces the option volume that originality requires per the divergence literatures; the structured-technique-systems where forced connection, reversal, exaggeration, substitution, and combination methods generate systematically beyond waiting for inspiration per the technique traditions; the creative-brief-response-methodology where tension, mandate, and single-minded proposition from the strategy sections channel ideation per the response traditions; the collaborative-ideation-craft where group sessions run facilitation discipline that individual ideation feeds per the collaboration literatures of the ecosystem section; the reference-and-swipe-discipline where category, cross-category, and historical work supplies pattern material whose copying boundary the ethics of originality govern per the reference traditions); the concept-development system (the idea-to-concept-progression where raw notions develop into executable territories with message, format, and campaign potential per the progression literatures; the big-idea-evaluation-criteria where concepts face tests of strategic fit, distinctiveness, emotional power,extension feasibility, and durability per the evaluation traditions; the concept-articulation-craft where ideas communicate through rationale documents, reference boards, and rough execution sketches per the articulation literatures; the concept-testing-integration where candidate territories face audience response research before full production per the testing traditions of the performance section); the execution-development practice (the copy-visual-joint-development where words and images develop together per the collaboration disciplines of the design section; the execution-variant-production where single concepts generate multiple executions testing range and resilience per the variant traditions; the craft-refinement-iteration where drafts progress through revision cycles with the distance-freshness scheduling per the refinement literatures; the presentation-preparation where work gets built for persuasive internal and client presentation per the presentation traditions of the ecosystem section); the creative-block-and-productivity management (the block-mechanism-understanding where fear, perfectionism, brief confusion, and exhaustion produce blocks whose diagnosis determines remedy per the block literatures; the daily-practice-discipline where regular production habit maintains creative capacity independent of inspiration per the practice traditions; the deadline-craft-realism where professional production under constraint develops the compression skills commercial work demands per the deadline literatures; the input-management-practice where diverse reading, observation, and experience maintain the recombination material per the input traditions); and the portfolio-and-growth practice (the portfolio-development-strategy where work accumulates demonstrating range, craft, and thinking per the portfolio traditions of the foundations section; the self-critique-development where evaluation skills turn inward through checklist and comparative analysis per the critique traditions; the peer-review-participation where work faces colleague challenge within the feedback cultures of the ecosystem section; the skill-frontier-expansion where new formats, tools including generative systems, and methods receive structured learning per the expansion traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the inspiration-waiter whose process depends on muse visits that professional schedules cannot accommodate, remediated by the methodology toolkit; the premature-converger whose first viable idea becomes the only idea because divergent volume never happened, remediated by the divergence practice; the brief-rebel whose concepts ignore strategic mandate in favor of interesting-to-maker work, remediated by the response methodology; the concept-inflator whose simple execution ideas get presented as big ideas lacking range and durability, remediated by the evaluation criteria; the single-draft-perfectionist whose work never iterates because first drafts face shame rather than revision, remediated by the refinement practice; and the portfolio-neglecting-professional whose years of work never assembled into capability evidence, remediated by the development strategy with detection methods as the diagnostic.
