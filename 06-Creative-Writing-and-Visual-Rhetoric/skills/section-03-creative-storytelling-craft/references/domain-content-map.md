## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the narrative discipline whose compelling-story mastery the course description names as central craft, grounded in story science rather than anecdote: the narrative-power foundation (the transportation-mechanism where story immersion suspends counter-arguing and produces attitude change through experience simulation rather than claim acceptance per the transportation literatures of the behavioral companion; the memory-advantage documentation where narrative structures encode and retrieve better than fact lists per the memory systems of the neuromarketing companion; the neural-coupling-evidence where listeners' brain patterns align with storytellers' during narrative, the synchronization research grounding story's persuasion per the coupling literatures; the stickiness-connection where stories function as the flight-simulator of the Heath framework's persuasion system per the Made to Stick traditions of the sources); the story-structure toolkit (the classical-arc-architecture where setup, confrontation, and resolution organize narrative energy with the three-act conventions commercial storytelling compresses per the structure literatures; the hero's-journey-pattern where departure, initiation, and return map transformation narratives whose brand application the StoryBrand framework operationalizes per the journey traditions; the StoryBrand-customer-hero-model where the customer functions as hero, the brand as guide providing plan and call-to-action, structured around problem, empathy, authority, success, and failure stakes per the framework traditions of the sources; the micro-story-forms where single-scene narratives, customer vignettes, and origin anecdotes deliver story power in short formats per the micro literatures); the narrative-element craft (the character-construction discipline where protagonists carry desire and obstacle whose specificity produces identification per the character literatures; the conflict-engine-principle where story runs on want-meets-obstacle tension whose absence produces chronicle rather than narrative per the conflict traditions; the stakes-articulation-craft where success and failure consequences give narrative urgency connecting to the loss-aversion mechanics of the behavioral companion per the stakes literatures; the transformation-arc-design where change from before-state to after-state constitutes the meaning payload whose product-connection must remain honest per the arc traditions; the scene-and-detail-craft where concrete sensory specificity creates presence per the concreteness traditions of the foundations section); the brand-narrative system (the origin-story-development where founding narratives carry values and authenticity signals with the truthfulness requirement the ethics section enforces per the origin literatures; the brand-as-character-construction where consistent personality, voice, and values make brands narrative agents per the character traditions connecting to the archetype systems of the anthropology companion; the customer-story-mining where authentic customer transformation narratives supply the most credible story inventory per the mining traditions connecting to the social-proof economics of the behavioral companion; the narrative-architecture-planning where story assets organize across channels and journey stages per the architecture traditions of the strategy companion sections); the format-adaptation practice (the long-form-narrative-craft where brand films, features, and campaign stories sustain structure across minutes per the long-form literatures of the format section; the short-form-compression discipline where social and spot narratives deliver arc essence in seconds per the compression literatures; the serialized-narrative-design where episodic structure builds audience across installments per the serialization traditions; the interactive-and-participatory-story forms where audience agency in narrative creates engagement depth with the coherence-control tradeoffs per the interactive literatures connecting to the co-creation traditions of the anthropology companion); and the story-strategy integration (the positioning-narrative-alignment where stories express the strategic positioning of the anthropology companion's tension territories rather than decorating arbitrary plots per the alignment literatures; the cultural-narrative-awareness where story patterns carry cultural variation whose local resonance the anthropology companion's global section governs per the cultural literatures; the narrative-measurement-connection where story performance faces the engagement and effectiveness testing of the performance section per the measurement traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the chronicle-mistaken-for-story whose brand history lists events without conflict, stakes, or transformation, encoding nothing, remediated by the conflict-engine principle; the brand-hero-hijacker whose narratives center the company as protagonist while customers watch, inverting the framework that makes story sell, remediated by the customer-hero model; the stakes-free-narrator whose pleasant stories carry no failure possibility and therefore no urgency, remediated by the stakes craft; the detail-starved-abstractionist whose stories stay generic because concrete scene work never happened, remediated by the scene craft; the origin-fabricator whose invented founding mythology faces exposure through records or former employees, remediated by the truthfulness requirement; and the format-monotone-storyteller whose feature-length structure gets crammed into short-form contexts or whose social fragments never build serial architecture, remediated by the adaptation practice with detection methods as the diagnostic.
