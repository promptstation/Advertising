## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the design literacy whose fundamentals writers and strategists must command to collaborate, evaluate, and direct visual work even when they do not execute it: the design-principle foundation (the hierarchy-principle where visual weight distribution asserts importance order, the layout expression of the argument sequencing in the rhetoric section per the hierarchy literatures; the alignment-and-grid-discipline where structural order produces the processing fluency whose preference effects the neuromarketing companion documents per the grid traditions; the contrast-and-emphasis-systems where difference in size, weight, color, and space creates focal logic per the contrast literatures; the proximity-and-grouping-principles where spatial relationship communicates logical relationship per the Gestalt traditions of the perception science; the white-space-function literacy where breathing room carries premium signal and comprehension support rather than constituting wasted area per the space literatures); the typography-communication system (the typeface-association-literacy where serif, sans, script, and display families carry tone and era associations per the type traditions connecting to the sound-symbolism parallels of the neuromarketing companion; the readability-mechanics where size, line length, line spacing, and contrast determine comprehension speed per the readability literatures; the typographic-hierarchy-craft where heading scales, weight variation, and style systems organize document and screen structure per the hierarchy traditions; the type-pairing-and-system-discipline where font combinations and variable usage form coherent systems per the system literatures; the brand-typography-identity where signature typefaces function as recognition assets per the identity traditions of the voice section); the layout-composition practice (the grid-system-application where column, modular, and baseline grids structure consistent composition per the grid traditions; the format-convention-literacy where ad units, social templates, packaging panels, and page layouts carry established expectation patterns per the convention literatures of the media companion; the responsive-and-adaptive-design-awareness where layouts restructure across screens and sizes with the hierarchy-preservation discipline per the responsive literatures; the information-density-judgment where content volume matches format capacity and audience processing willingness per the density traditions connecting to the cognitive-load systems of the behavioral companion); the image-text-integration craft (the copy-space-collaboration discipline where writing and layout develop together because copy length and visual weight interlock per the collaboration traditions of the ecosystem section; the caption-and-label-systems where text elements anchor visual meaning per the anchor literatures of the rhetoric section; the CTA-design-integration where action elements receive visual treatment matching function importance per the action traditions of the copy section; the multi-element-balance-judgment where headline, image, body, logo, and legal elements distribute attention per the balance literatures); the design-evaluation-and-direction practice (the critique-vocabulary-development where non-designers acquire precise language for visual feedback, transforming taste reactions into actionable direction per the critique traditions of the collaboration section; the design-brief-craft where visual requirements communicate through reference, constraint, and rationale per the brief traditions of the process section; the quality-assessment-checklists where alignment, hierarchy, contrast, spacing, and typographic consistency face systematic review per the assessment traditions; the trend-and-convention-awareness where contemporary visual styles and platform conventions inform without dictating per the convention literatures of the anthropology companion); and the tool-literacy foundation (the accessible-design-tools landscape where template systems and lightweight tools enable non-designer production within quality bounds per the tooling literatures; the professional-workflow-awareness where designer tools, file formats, and production stages structure collaboration per the workflow traditions; the asset-management-basics where versions, formats, and rights organize per the management literatures of the operations traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the hierarchy-flattener whose layouts present all elements at equal weight, arguing nothing and guiding nobody, remediated by the hierarchy principle; the readability-sacrificing-stylist whose type choices serve mood while destroying comprehension, remediated by the mechanics literacy; the white-space-fearer whose crowded layouts signal discount and confuse processing, remediated by the space literacy; the taste-only-critiquer whose design feedback runs on like and dislike vocabulary that gives direction nothing to execute, remediated by the critique vocabulary; the copy-thrower-over-wall whose word counts ignore spatial reality until design butchers message priority, remediated by the collaboration discipline; and the template-fossilizer whose brand visuals inherit template defaults because convention awareness never developed, remediated by the trend and quality practices with detection methods as the diagnostic.
