## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the ethical discipline whose truth-in-advertising obligations bound all persuasive craft, protecting consumers, clients, and the profession's legitimacy: the truth-in-advertising foundation (the deception-standard-framework where material claims must be truthful and substantiated under the FTC traditions of the sources, with the reasonable-consumer interpretation principle governing how claims get evaluated per the regulatory literatures; the substantiation-discipline where objective product claims require evidence before dissemination, the documentation requirement that shapes copy practice per the substantiation traditions; the materiality-principle where deception law focuses on claims likely to affect purchase decisions per the materiality literatures; the puffery-boundary literacy where subjective exaggeration enjoys legal tolerance with the modern erosion of that tolerance through consumer sophistication and platform policy per the boundary literatures); the claim-craft-within-bounds (the claim-precision-discipline where qualifiers, comparatives, superlatives, and demonstration claims receive accuracy engineering per the precision literatures; the testimonial-and-endorsement-standards where representative results, disclosure of relationships, and genuine experience govern social proof execution per the endorsement traditions connecting to the behavioral companion's proof systems; the price-and-offer-honesty where reference prices, discount claims, and terms reflect genuine economics per the pricing-transparency literatures of the behavioral companion; the visual-claim-integrity where imagery including before-after presentations, food styling, and product rendering faces the same truth standards as words per the visual-truth traditions of the rhetoric section); the manipulation-boundary application (the dark-pattern-copy-discipline where confirmshaming language, forced-continuity terms, and misdirection copy face the dark-pattern taxonomy of the behavioral companion per the pattern literatures; the vulnerability-awareness-craft where persuasive pressure on financially strained, health-anxious, elderly, and young audiences carries heightened ethical weight per the vulnerability literatures across the companion courses; the fear-and-pressure-limits where urgency and threat appeals operate within the calibration and honesty boundaries documented across the curriculum per the limit traditions; the subliminal-and-implicit-claims-honesty where implied messages face the same material-truth standards as explicit claims per the implication literatures connecting to the neuromarketing companion's myth corrections); the social-responsibility dimension (the stereotype-and-representation-responsibility where creative choices carry the representation standards of the anthropology companion per the responsibility literatures; the body-image-and-aspiration-effects where beauty, success, and lifestyle portrayal accumulates social influence whose harm patterns the industry increasingly acknowledges per the effects literatures; the health-and-environment-claim-rigor where wellness, sustainability, and green positioning face substantiation and greenwashing enforcement per the claim literatures; the children-and-protected-audience-standards where advertising to minors operates under heightened restriction per the protection traditions); the intellectual-property-and-originality ethics (the plagiarism-and-appropriation-boundaries where copying, close reference, and homage face legal and professional standards per the originality literatures of the process section; the rights-and-licensing-discipline where image, music, font, and talent rights govern production per the rights traditions of the art-direction section; the AI-generated-content-questions where generative-tool output raises disclosure, rights, and originality issues whose standards continue forming per the emerging literatures; the credit-and-attribution-practice where collaborative work carries honest acknowledgment per the credit traditions of the ecosystem section); the professional-integrity system (the code-and-standard-ecosystem where industry associations, platform policies, and regulatory guidance define professional expectation per the code traditions across the companion courses; the internal-advocacy-practice where creatives raise ethical concerns within client and agency processes per the advocacy traditions of the ecosystem section; the client-ethics-navigation where problematic briefs face pushback, reframe, or refusal with the career-reality honesty per the navigation literatures; the personal-standard-development where individual ethical commitments mature beyond compliance minimums per the standard traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the substantiation-afterthought-claimer whose campaign ships assertions that evidence review would have qualified, converting creativity into liability, remediated by the discipline framework; the puffery-reliance-defender whose material claims hide in exaggeration language until enforcement reclassifies them, remediated by the boundary literacy; the visual-liar whose imagery demonstrates results the product cannot deliver, remediated by the visual-integrity standards; the dark-pattern-copywriter whose conversion optimization deploys shame and concealment language, remediated by the manipulation-boundary application; the stereotype-convenience-user whose deadline pressure defaults to representational shorthand that harms and dates, remediated by the responsibility dimension; and the integrity-flexible-professional whose ethical standards bend with budget pressure until exposure redefines their reputation, remediated by the professional-integrity system with detection methods as the diagnostic.
