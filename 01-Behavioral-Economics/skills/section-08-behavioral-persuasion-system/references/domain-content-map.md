## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Completes the Cialdini persuasion framework whose remaining principles — reciprocity, commitment and consistency, authority, liking, and unity — combine with the scarcity and social proof of prior sections into the full influence system underlying campaign design: the system overview (the principle-arsenal concept where seven mechanisms with distinct psychological foundations equip different campaign problems, reciprocity opening relationships, commitment deepening them, authority and liking building credibility and affinity, unity forging identity bonds, social proof and scarcity accelerating decision per the influence literatures; the combination discipline where principles sequence across journey stages rather than stack simultaneously, matching mechanism to relationship depth and decision state; the pre-suasion concept where attention direction before the message — the question asked, the image shown, the context set — shapes which principles will land per the pre-suasion traditions); reciprocity engineering (the gift-first dynamic where unrequested value creates obligation pressure, operating through free samples whose purchase-lift effects are documented, generous content marketing whose education precedes ask, and tool-style lead magnets whose utility exceeds typical gated-content exchange per the reciprocity literatures; the concession pattern where reciprocal compromise following initial ask shapes negotiation-style offer sequencing including discount-on-abandonment flows; the personalization multiplier where tailored gifts and favors strengthen obligation beyond generic ones per the customization research; the reciprocity boundary where excessive or guilt-laden giving triggers reactance and where undisclosed quid pro quo crosses into manipulation per the ethics frameworks); commitment and consistency mechanics (the foot-in-the-door sequence where small initial compliance raises subsequent larger-request acceptance through self-perception and consistency pressure per the compliance literatures; the micro-conversion architecture where quiz answers, email capture, configurator engagement, and sample requests accumulate small commitments that reshape self-concept toward buyer identity per the funnel-psychology traditions; the public-commitment effect where declared intentions including wishlist creation, waitlist joining, and social sharing raise follow-through per the commitment research; the written-and-active advantage where effortful self-generated commitment outpersuades passive receipt per the generation-effect literatures; the consistency-branding application where brands that behave predictably across touchpoints earn the consistency-trust that makes commitment safe per the brand-coherence traditions); authority construction (the credential-display system where certifications, degrees, institutional affiliations, and years-in-business signal competence per the authority literatures whose foundational demonstrations show symbol-level authority cues shifting compliance; the earned-media validation where press logos, awards, and independent ratings borrow institutional authority per the third-party-endorsement research; the expertise-content strategy where demonstrated knowledge in published material establishes authority more durably than claimed titles per the content-marketing traditions; the authority-truthfulness standard where borrowed credentials must be genuine and relevant because fabricated authority constitutes deception under advertising law per the substantiation frameworks); liking cultivation (the similarity principle where shared attributes, language, and values raise influence, driving representation, vernacular, and community-fit decisions in creative per the similarity literatures; the familiarity-and-cooperation pattern where repeated pleasant contact and collaborative interaction including co-creation build affinity per the mere-exposure and cooperation research; the compliment-and-appreciation effects where sincere recognition raises regard with the authenticity boundary; the association craft where attractive endorsers, pleasant aesthetics, and positive contexts transfer affect to brand per the classical-conditioning traditions; the brand-personality discipline where consistent character traits make brands likeable entities rather than faceless sellers per the brand-personality literatures); unity and shared identity (the we-principle where perceived oneness through family, tribe, place, fandom, or cause activates identity-based influence exceeding liking per the unity literatures; the community-architecture practice where brand communities, insider language, member rituals, and co-creation convert customers into identity-holders whose defense of the brand exceeds any campaign claim; the identity-congruent-positioning discipline where products enter existing self-concepts rather than asking consumers to adopt new ones per the identity-based-motivation research; the unity-shadow awareness where in-group bonding through out-group derogation creates polarization risk and excluded-audience damage per the tribal-marketing cautions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the transactional-reciprocator whose gifts arrive with visible strings converting generosity into pressure and reactance, remediated by the reciprocity boundary; the commitment-escalator whose micro-yes ladders push consumers toward purchases contradicting their interests, converting consistency mechanics into entrapment claims, remediated by the ethics frameworks; the borrowed-authority fabricator whose fake credentials surface in diligence and press scrutiny, remediated by the truthfulness standard; the flattery-spammer whose generic compliments and manufactured similarity read as manipulation to persuasion-literate audiences, remediated by the authenticity conditions; the unity-polarizer whose tribe-building depends on mocking outsiders, accumulating reputational fragility, remediated by the unity-shadow awareness; and the principle-stuffer whose single message deploys all seven mechanisms visibly, triggering the persuasion-knowledge skepticism documented across sections, remediated by the combination discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
