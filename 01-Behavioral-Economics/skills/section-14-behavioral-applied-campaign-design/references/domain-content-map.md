## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Architect complete behavioral campaigns through brief methodology (behavioral-document formats, behavior definition, mechanism-budget alignment), archetype libraries (DTC, subscription, considered-purchase, FMCG, behavior-change patterns), cross-channel orchestration (frame continuity, frequency planning, proof chains, retargeting diagnosis), case analysis (mechanism decoding, failure post-mortems, category transfer), program integration (behavioral audits.
2. Build-measure-learn cycles, stakeholder communication), and craft judgment (selection wisdom, restraint discipline, brand coherence), avoiding tactics without strategy, archetype blindness, frame flipping, case copying, lever maximalism.
3. Audit avoidance failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the integration discipline where all thirteen prior sections assemble into working campaign architecture across the major commercial contexts: the behavioral-brief methodology (the brief-as-behavioral-document format where campaign briefs specify target behavior and audience decision state, the operative mechanisms with evidence basis, selected principles and their journey-stage placement, choice-architecture decisions on defaults and option structure, proof and framing plans, pricing and offer psychology, guardrails and ethics review, and test hypotheses — replacing demographic-and-message-only briefs per the behavioral-planning traditions; the behavior-definition discipline where campaign objectives state observable behavior change with baseline and target rather than attitude aspirations per the objective-setting literatures; the mechanism-budget alignment where media weight, creative investment, and offer economics distribute according to the identified decision dynamics rather than convention); the campaign-archetype libraries (the direct-to-consumer-commerce archetype where new-brand launch sequences fluency-optimized awareness through social-proof-heavy consideration into scarcity-and-risk-reversal conversion with post-purchase peak-end onboarding, exercising proof, scarcity, framing, guarantee, and journey sections; the subscription-service archetype where trial endowment design, payment-decoupling ethics, habit-cue installation, tier architecture, and cancellation-fairness discipline combine per the retention section; the considered-purchase and B2B archetype where authority construction, commitment ladders from content engagement through configuration to consultation, and case-study proof dominate across long multi-stakeholder journeys per the persuasion and journey sections; the fast-moving-consumer-goods archetype where availability salience, packaging fluency, price-ending conventions, habit-cue placement, and promotion psychology operate at low-involvement speed per the heuristic and pricing sections; the behavior-change and nonprofit archetype where loss framing, social-norm messaging, commitment devices, and friction reduction serve outcomes from health to sustainability with the heightened ethics review per the nudge-policy traditions); the cross-channel behavioral-orchestration (the frame-continuity discipline where the psychological frame — gain versus loss, identity versus utility, urgency versus assurance — persists across ad, landing, email, and checkout because frame-switching resets processing per the coherence traditions of the journey section; the frequency-and-fluency planning where exposure schedules serve mere-exposure preference building within wear-out bounds per the repetition literatures; the proof-chain sequencing where social proof, authority, and reciprocity deploy in escalating commitment order across touchpoints per the pre-suasion and combination disciplines; the retargeting-behavioral-design where abandonment recovery applies mechanism diagnosis — distraction gets reminder, distrust gets proof, price shock gets reframe or offer — rather than uniform repetition per the retargeting literatures); the case-analysis methodology (the mechanism-decoding practice where successful published campaigns get reverse-engineered into their behavioral components, building the pattern library that informs original design per the case-method traditions; the failure-post-mortem discipline where backfired campaigns — reactance-triggering pressure, tone-deaf personalization, scarcity exposed as fake — get mechanism-level diagnosis per the failure-literature traditions; the category-transfer judgment where mechanisms tested in one vertical get assessed for boundary-condition fit before application in another per the context-qualification habits of the foundations section); the program-level integration (the behavioral-audit engagement where existing campaigns and funnels receive full-diagnostic review against the section frameworks producing prioritized intervention roadmaps per the audit traditions; the build-measure-learn cycle operation where campaign launches carry embedded test plans whose results feed iteration per the experimentation section; the stakeholder-communication craft where behavioral rationale translates into briefings that executives, creative teams, and media planners can act on per the professional-communication traditions); and the craft-judgment synthesis (the principle-selection wisdom where experience accumulates into fast pattern-matching about which mechanisms fit which contexts, the expertise that frameworks serve rather than replace per the expertise literatures; the restraint discipline where the strongest campaigns typically deploy two or three mechanisms coherently rather than every available lever, honoring the persuasion-knowledge limits of audiences per the cognitive-load and reactance traditions; the brand-behavioral-coherence standard where behavioral techniques express brand character — luxury scarcity differs from value scarcity, playful unity differs from serious authority — per the brand-integration literatures) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the tactics-without-strategy assembler whose campaign collects interesting techniques without behavior definition or mechanism rationale, producing activity without theory, remediated by the brief methodology; the archetype-blind improviser whose subscription offer borrows FMCG mechanics ignoring consideration depth, remediated by the archetype libraries; the frame-flipper whose retargeting contradicts the originating ad's psychological frame, resetting accumulated processing at every touch, remediated by the continuity discipline; the case-copier whose replication of a celebrated campaign ignores the category, era, and audience conditions that made it work, remediated by the transfer judgment; the lever-maximalist whose every-mechanism campaigns trigger audience skepticism and internal incoherence, remediated by the restraint discipline; and the audit-avoider whose live funnels never face systematic behavioral review because launch velocity crowds out diagnosis, remediated by the program-integration practice with detection methods as the diagnostic.
