## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Organize campaigns behaviorally through segmentation foundations (demographic insufficiency, decision styles, involvement dimensions, risk orientation, context segments), persona reconstruction (behavior-first formats, research foundations, mechanism mapping), journey-stage psychology (awareness through advocacy dynamics).
2. Audit methodology (stage inspection, drop-off diagnosis, moments of truth, cross-channel coherence), progression mechanics (goal gradient, endowed progress, intention capture, procrastination countermeasures), and orchestration practice (lifecycle sequencing, segment-journey matrices, measurement integration), avoiding demographic personas, decorative maps, stage blindness, channel discontinuity, and mid-journey switching failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the segmentation and journey discipline where behavioral principles become organized campaign architecture by matching psychological state to funnel stage: the behavioral-segmentation foundation (the demographic-insufficiency principle where age, gender, and income describe populations but not decision machinery, requiring segmentation on how people choose rather than who they are per the behavioral-segmentation literatures; the decision-style segments including maximizers who exhaustively compare and satisficers who accept good-enough options per the Schwartz traditions, with different information needs, proof requirements, and abandonment patterns; the involvement-and-knowledge dimensions where category expertise and personal relevance determine processing depth per the elaboration frameworks of the heuristic section; the risk-orientation segments where loss-sensitive, quality-anxious, and switch-happy consumers require differentiated guarantee, proof, and retention treatments; the occasion-and-context segmentation where the same person decides differently under time pressure, gifting contexts, and habitual repurchase per the situational literatures); the persona-reconstruction practice (the behavior-first persona format where documents lead with decision patterns, triggers, objections, proof requirements, and bias susceptibilities before demographics per the modern-persona literatures; the research-foundation discipline where personas derive from journey analytics, survey data, session observation, and interview evidence rather than stereotype assembly per the research traditions; the persona-mechanism mapping where each segment carries documented dominant heuristics and predictable response patterns enabling principle selection per campaign); the journey-stage psychology (the awareness-stage dynamics where attention economics, mere-exposure seeding, and fluency-optimized first impressions operate on consumers who are not yet choosing per the attention literatures; the consideration-stage dynamics where social proof, authority, comparison architecture, and objection handling serve active evaluation per the proof and architecture sections; the decision-stage dynamics where scarcity, urgency, risk-reversal, default structure, and friction removal serve the conversion moment per the prior sections; the onboarding-stage dynamics where peak-end design, early-win engineering, and habit-cue installation determine whether purchase becomes retention per the experience literatures; the advocacy-stage dynamics where identity investment, community belonging, and sharing mechanics convert satisfaction into proof generation closing the loop per the unity and UGC traditions); the journey-audit methodology (the stage-by-stage behavioral inspection where each transition faces analysis of psychological state, dominant biases, current architecture, and friction inventory per the audit traditions of the choice-architecture section; the drop-off-psychology diagnosis where abandonment points receive mechanism-based interpretation distinguishing overload, distrust, distraction, price shock, and friction per the funnel-diagnostic literatures; the moment-of-truth identification where decisions concentrate at search, shelf, checkout, delivery, and first-use touchpoints per the consumer-journey traditions including the zero-moment-of-truth digital-search extension; the cross-channel-coherence review where behavioral treatment stays consistent across paid, owned, and earned touchpoints because mechanism-switching mid-journey resets accumulated commitment and fluency per the consistency disciplines); the progression-mechanics toolkit (the goal-gradient effect where motivation accelerates near completion, structuring progress bars, loyalty tracking, and multi-step flows per the gradient literatures; the endowed-progress technique where artificial starting points — two of ten stamps pre-given — raise completion rates per the coffee-card experiments; the intention-capture practice where plan-making prompts and implementation-intention questions convert vague interest into scheduled behavior per the intention literatures; the procrastination-countermeasures where deadlines, default scheduling, and loss-framed delay costs address the intention-action gap per the present-bias traditions); and the journey-orchestration practice (the lifecycle-sequencing discipline where mechanism deployment follows relationship depth, proof and reciprocity early, commitment and unity later, scarcity at decision points per the combination disciplines of the persuasion section; the segment-journey matrix where behavioral segments meet journey stages defining differentiated path design per the personalization traditions; the measurement-integration where journey-stage metrics connect to the experimentation systems of the following sections) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the demographic-persona shop whose segments describe thirty-five-year-old females sharing no decision pattern, producing campaigns that match nobody's psychology, remediated by the behavior-first format; the journey-map-decoration practice whose pretty stage diagrams never carry bias analysis or architecture audit into campaign decisions, remediated by the audit methodology; the stage-blind blaster whose single pressure-heavy message hits awareness-stage consumers who needed fluency and exposure instead, remediated by the stage-psychology literacy; the channel-discontinuous orchestrator whose landing page contradicts the ad's frame and proof, resetting trust at every handoff, remediated by the coherence review; and the mid-journey-switcher whose mechanism changes between consideration and decision break accumulated commitment, remediated by the sequencing discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
