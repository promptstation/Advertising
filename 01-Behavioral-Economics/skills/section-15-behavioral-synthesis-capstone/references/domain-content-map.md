## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Consolidates the complete discipline from rational-model departure through bias taxonomy, heuristics, prospect theory, scarcity, social proof, choice architecture, persuasion systems, pricing psychology, journey design, habit and loyalty, ethics and regulation, experimentation, and campaign integration into the unified behavioral-economics capability the course description names as the foundation of any high-converting campaign: the discipline synthesis — the complete mechanism-to-campaign chain (the end-to-end architecture where decision-machinery literacy generates mechanism hypotheses, hypotheses select principles with boundary conditions, principles shape choice architecture and offer design, designs deploy across journey stages with frame continuity, deployments face experimentation with guardrails, verified levers scale within ethical boundaries, and feedback loops return learning to the mechanism library, every section operating as one system; the science-craft-ethics triad where empirical literacy, design skill, and boundary discipline combine as the distinctive competency this course certifies, each insufficient alone — science without craft produces inert knowledge, craft without science produces folklore, both without ethics produce liability; the humility-foundation where the replication landscape, effect-size realism, and context-dependence remind the practitioner that behavioral science informs judgment rather than replacing it per the evidence-grading traditions of the foundations section); the mechanism-command integration (the bias-and-heuristic fluency where the full taxonomy serves as diagnostic vocabulary for reading any consumer decision context per the middle sections; the architecture command where defaults, option sets, frames, friction, pricing psychology, and proof systems combine as the design surface per the choice, pricing, and persuasion sections; the journey-and-habit command where mechanisms sequence across funnel stages and compound into automaticity and loyalty per the journey and retention sections; the verification command where every claimed lever faces mechanism-hypothesis testing with guardrails and replication per the experimentation section); the diagnostic mastery — reading campaigns, funnels, and offers behaviorally (the funnel-audit protocol where any customer journey faces stage-by-stage inspection of processing fluency, proof adequacy, architecture effects, friction inventory, and ethics compliance synthesizing all sections' failure libraries into the diagnostic reference; the offer-audit protocol where any price and terms structure faces reference-point analysis, prospect framing, payment psychology, and risk-reversal assessment per the pricing section; the campaign-audit protocol where any creative and media plan faces mechanism identification, frame coherence, audience-processing-depth fit, and guardrail review per the integration section; the dark-pattern-audit protocol where any interface faces the taxonomy inspection distinguishing sludge, deception, and legitimate design per the ethics section); the capstone project — demonstrating integrated mastery (the capstone specification requiring candidates to produce a complete behavioral campaign strategy for a real product or service: a behavioral research foundation documenting decision context, segment decision styles, and journey-stage psychology; a mechanism-selection rationale connecting chosen principles to evidence and boundary conditions; a full choice-architecture design covering option structure, defaults, frames, pricing psychology, and proof systems; a cross-channel journey orchestration with frame continuity and lifecycle sequencing; a measurement plan with mechanism-hypothesis tests, guardrail metrics, and incrementality approach; a completed ethics review applying the publicity and welfare tests with any dark-pattern risks documented and resolved; and a defense document anticipating the strongest objections to each design choice; the evidence-integration requirement where design decisions cite the course's source literatures and documented effect sizes rather than assertion; the evaluation criteria covering mechanism-selection soundness, architecture coherence, measurement rigor, ethics discipline, and defense quality; the portfolio development where capstone work becomes the practitioner's demonstration artifact for strategy roles); the professional-identity synthesis (the behavioral-strategist identity where consumer-science literacy, campaign-craft skill, experimentation discipline, and ethics commitment combine into the professional who designs for actual human decision machinery rather than imaginary rational agents; the skeptic-practitioner paradox mastery where enthusiasm for powerful levers coexists with adversarial testing and boundary respect, the productive tension defining mature practice; the consumer-respect orientation where the persuasion-manipulation line functions as professional identity rather than constraint, per the ethics section; the continuous-updating commitment where the replication-driven evolution of behavioral science demands ongoing literature engagement per the lifelong-learning traditions); and the forward path (the discipline-frontier awareness covering privacy-era targeting evolution toward contextual and zero-party-data strategies, AI-driven personalization whose behavioral-design questions scale automatically, tightening dark-pattern regulation across jurisdictions, and the maturation of behavioral science within platform and agency practice whose trajectories reshape capability boundaries; the specialization routes toward conversion-rate optimization, loyalty and retention design, behavioral public policy, or research leadership building on this foundation; the companion-course connections where neuromarketing deepens the subconscious layer, predictive analytics supplies the data machinery, media mathematics delivers the targeting engineering, cultural anthropology contextualizes across populations, and creative craft executes the message per the curriculum family; the capability-development planning against this course's fifteen-section skill inventory with deliberate practice on identified gaps) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the bias-collector whose taxonomy knowledge never became architecture, testing, or campaign skill, remediated by the capstone's full-chain requirement; the lever-enthusiast-without-evidence whose designs cite principles but never faced guardrailed testing, remediated by the verification command; the ethics-postponer whose conversion focus treated boundaries as compliance afterthought until enforcement or exposure forced reckoning, remediated by the identity integration; the framework-idolater whose mechanical section-application ignored the craft judgment that contexts demand, remediated by the humility foundation; the capstone-as-deck whose strategy document lacks evidence integration and defense preparation, remediated by the authenticity requirements; and the destination-illusion treating course completion as mastery arrival rather than the start of test-by-test, campaign-by-campaign calibration, remediated by the continuous-updating commitment with detection methods as the diagnostic.
