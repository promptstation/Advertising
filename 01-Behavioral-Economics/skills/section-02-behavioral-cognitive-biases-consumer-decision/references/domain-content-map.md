## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Maps the full bias taxonomy whose predictable deviations from rational judgment constitute the advertiser's working knowledge of consumer error and shortcut: the bias concept itself (the definition as systematic rather than random deviation, where errors run in consistent directions across populations making them exploitable for prediction and design; the distinction between biases as cognitive failures and biases as efficient approximations that serve well in ancestral environments but misfire in market environments filled with engineered stimuli; the interaction principle where biases compound within single decisions and across journey stages rather than operating in isolation per the judgment research traditions); the judgment and estimation family (the anchoring bias where initial values distort subsequent estimates, operating in advertised reference prices, opening offers, and even irrelevant numerical cues, with the documented persistence despite awareness per the anchoring literatures; the availability heuristic's bias dimension where vivid, recent, or emotionally charged instances dominate probability judgment, shaping risk perception of product categories and recall-driven brand evaluation; the representativeness bias where similarity judgments override base rates, driving consumer inference that premium packaging means premium contents or that a health-halo attribute means overall healthfulness; the affect heuristic where current feeling states substitute for attribute analysis in brand and product evaluation per the feeling-as-information research); the memory and experience family (the peak-end rule where experiences get evaluated by their most intense moment and their ending rather than their average, structuring unboxing design, customer-service recovery, and campaign finales; the hindsight bias where post-purchase outcome knowledge distorts memory of prior uncertainty, complicating consumer research that asks people to recall decision reasons; the serial-position effects including primacy and recency where ad order, feature order, and option order carry measurable evaluation weight); the social and self-relevant family (the bandwagon effect where perceived popularity breeds further adoption, the engine behind bestseller badges and trend marketing; the halo effect where single positive attributes or attractive endorsers spill into global evaluation of brand and product; the overconfidence and optimism biases where consumers overestimate their ability to use complex products, redeem offers, or stick to subscriptions, a fact that both enables certain offer structures and creates the ethical and regulatory exposure documented in dark-pattern enforcement; the planning fallacy where time and cost underestimation shapes response to deadlines and project-based purchases; the confirmation bias where post-choice information search favors the decision already made, sustaining brand loyalty and shaping review-reading behavior); the bias-application methodology (the journey-mapping technique where each funnel stage gets audited for which biases naturally operate and which the campaign can legitimately engage; the mechanism-matching discipline where the selected bias connects to the actual decision context rather than being layered on decoratively; the effect-size realism where published bias magnitudes inform but never guarantee field performance, requiring the testing programs of the measurement section; the debiasing counterpart where some contexts including high-involvement financial or health purchases call for helping consumers decide well rather than exploiting error, building the trust that sustains brands); and the bias-ethics boundary (the manipulation spectrum where identical mechanics range from helpful simplification through persuasion to exploitation depending on transparency, consumer benefit, and reversibility per the ethics frameworks of the later section) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the checklist applier who stacks five biases onto one landing page producing a carnival of pressure cues that triggers suspicion and reactance instead of conversion, remediated by the mechanism-matching discipline; the awareness-immune claimant who assumes naming a bias to consumers neutralizes it despite anchoring and other biases persisting through transparency, remediated by the empirical literature; the base-rate-ignoring segmenter whose persona construction treats vivid customer stories as representative against statistical realities, remediated by the representativeness literacy; the peak-end-neglect designer whose journeys spike negative affect at checkout and delivery, the exact moments memory weights most, remediated by the experience-family application; and the exploitation-slider whose bias engagement quietly crosses from simplification into harm, accumulating chargebacks, complaints, and regulatory attention, remediated by the ethics boundary with detection methods as the diagnostic.
