## Absorbed capabilities this module must produce
1. Architect choice environments through the architect's premise (no-neutral-design principle, architect role, libertarian paternalism, environment-over-willpower insight), default systems (opt-out asymmetry, default mechanisms, commercial catalog.
2. Design ethics), option-set engineering (assortment sizing, tier architecture, decoy techniques, attribute alignment), salience levers (ordering effects, grouping craft, disclosure timing, friction calibration), nudge toolkits (MINDSPACE and EAST systems, behaviour diagnosis, context matching).
3. Audit governance (architecture audits, sludge identification, transparency posture), avoiding accidental architecture, default abuse, choice overload, clumsy decoys, and uniform friction failures.

## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the choice-architecture discipline whose structuring of decision environments predictably shapes behavior, the applied core of the nudge tradition named in the course description: the architect's premise (the no-neutral-design principle where every presentation of options — ordering, grouping, defaults, framing — influences choice whether designed or not, making architecture a responsibility rather than an option per the nudge traditions; the choice-architect role where advertisers, merchants, and platform designers function as architects whose constructions determine which behaviors are easy, salient, and default; the libertarian-paternalism framing where preserving freedom of choice while steering toward better outcomes defines legitimate nudge design versus coercive manipulation per the policy literatures; the environment-over-willpower insight where restructuring context outperforms exhortation because behavior follows the path of least resistance per the behavioral-change traditions); the default-effect system (the opt-out versus opt-in asymmetry where default participation in retirement plans, organ donation, subscriptions, and marketing consent produces order-of-magnitude behavior differences among otherwise identical populations per the default-effect literatures; the default mechanisms combining status-quo bias, effort avoidance, and implied-endorsement reading where consumers treat defaults as recommended options; the commercial default catalog including pre-selected add-ons, auto-renewal enrollment, default privacy settings, and preset configurations whose conversion power carries matched ethical and regulatory scrutiny per the dark-pattern frameworks; the default-design ethics where beneficial defaults with easy opt-out separate nudge from sludge per the welfare standards of the ethics section); the option-set engineering (the assortment-size research where the jam-study lineage and subsequent meta-analytic work establish that large assortments attract but can inhibit purchase, with moderate sets and structured navigation optimizing both per the choice-overload literatures; the tier-architecture conventions where good-better-best structures anchor comparison and direct choice toward the middle via the compromise effect and away from extremes via extremeness aversion per the tiering research; the decoy and asymmetric-dominance technique where an inferior option positioned against a target makes the target visibly superior, the documented mechanism of subscription-table design per the decoy literatures; the attribute-alignment discipline where option sets differ on dimensions consumers actually value, avoiding comparison difficulty that defers decision per the choice-difficulty research); the salience and structure levers (the ordering effects where first and last positions carry recall and choice advantages per the serial-position traditions connecting to the bias section; the categorization and grouping craft where organized options reduce load and signal curation per the information-architecture literatures; the disclosure-timing and placement decisions where costs, terms, and conditions surface at the moment of relevance rather than buried at journey end, the line between clean design and drip-pricing deception per the pricing-transparency regulations; the friction-calibration discipline where desired behaviors get frictionless paths while harmful impulsivity meets deliberate speed bumps including cooling-off periods and confirmation steps per the sludge-audit traditions); the nudge framework toolkits (the MINDSPACE and EAST systems from the behavioural-insights practice where messenger effects, incentives, norms, defaults, salience, priming, affect, commitments, and ego levers organize intervention design per the policy-unit traditions; the behaviour-diagnosis method where target behavior decomposes into capability, opportunity, and motivation components determining which nudge family applies per the behaviour-change-wheel literatures; the context-matching judgment where interventions fit the actual decision environment including device, time pressure, and emotional state); and the audit-and-governance practice (the architecture audit protocol where existing journeys get walked step-by-step cataloguing every default, order, frame, and friction with behavior-effect assessment; the sludge-identification discipline where accumulated friction serving organizational convenience rather than consumer welfare gets flagged for removal per the sludge literatures; the nudge-transparency posture where architectures withstand public description, the practical test separating nudge from manipulation per the publicity-principle traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the accidental architect whose unplanned defaults and orderings quietly suppress conversion while nobody audits the environment, remediated by the audit protocol; the default-abuser whose pre-ticked add-ons and buried opt-outs accumulate chargebacks and enforcement exposure, remediated by the default-ethics standards; the choice-overloader whose mega-assortment and unstructured comparison defer purchase into abandonment, remediated by the option-set engineering; the decoy-clumsy designer whose obvious manipulation attempts appear in consumer reviews and social screenshots as brand damage, remediated by the subtlety and transparency posture; and the friction-uniform operator whose one-speed journey design applies equal friction everywhere instead of calibrating to behavior desirability, remediated by the calibration discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
