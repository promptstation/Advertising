## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the machine-learning core whose classification and regression models power the propensity, value, and response predictions the course description's forecasting promise depends on: the modeling framework (the supervised-learning structure where labeled historical outcomes train models to predict future cases, with the label-quality insight that models inherit the flaws of whatever counted as conversion, churn, or trend in the training data per the data-science literatures; the target-definition discipline where the prediction problem gets specified before algorithm selection — who converts, who churns, what sells, how much — because vague targets produce unvalidatable models per the problem-framing traditions; the feature-construction craft where raw events, customer attributes, campaign exposures, and time aggregations transform into predictors whose engineering typically matters more than algorithm choice per the feature literatures; the train-validation-test architecture where temporal splits respecting the arrow of time prevent the leakage that makes models spectacular in development and useless in production per the validation traditions); the algorithm toolkit (the logistic-regression baseline where interpretable coefficients predict probabilities with the regularization discipline that handles correlated marketing features per the regression literatures; the tree-ensemble methods where random forests and gradient boosting dominate tabular marketing prediction with documented accuracy advantages whose interpretation tools including feature importance and partial dependence partially restore transparency per the ensemble literatures; the linear-and-regularized-regression family for continuous targets including order value and demand quantities per the regression traditions; the algorithm-selection judgment where simple interpretable models win when data is thin, decisions need explanation, and incremental accuracy lacks commercial value, while ensembles win when data is rich and prediction quality directly drives spend per the model-selection traditions); the evaluation discipline (the classification-metrics suite where accuracy misleads under the extreme class imbalance of marketing response, demanding precision, recall, area-under-curve, and lift-based measures whose business interpretation connects to targeting economics per the evaluation literatures; the regression-metrics discipline where error measures face scale, skew, and outlier sensitivity requiring multiple complementary statistics per the error traditions; the baseline-comparison requirement where every model must beat naive predictions — overall rate, recency rules, single-feature heuristics — because unbenchmarked accuracy claims are meaningless per the benchmark traditions; the calibration-assessment practice where predicted probabilities match observed frequencies, the property that makes scores usable for expected-value decisions rather than mere ranking per the calibration literatures; the overfitting-detection system where train-test performance gaps, learning curves, and cross-validation stability reveal memorization per the overfitting traditions of the data-science framework); the marketing-application patterns (the propensity-scoring architecture where purchase, response, and engagement probability models drive targeting, personalization, and bid adjustment per the response-modeling literatures; the uplift-modeling concept where the causal question of who responds because of treatment differs from who responds anyway, requiring experiment-based training data per the uplift literatures connecting to the incrementality section; the lookalike-and-similarity mechanics where seed-audience expansion operates with the seed-quality and platform-transparency limitations per the audience literatures of the media companion; the scoring-deployment patterns where batch scoring, real-time inference, and platform-integrated activation deliver predictions into campaign systems per the deployment traditions); the model-lifecycle management (the drift-monitoring discipline where feature distributions, prediction patterns, and performance metrics shift as audiences, platforms, and seasons evolve, demanding surveillance schedules per the drift literatures; the retraining-governance where model refresh follows documented cadence and validation rather than silent decay or chaotic tinkering per the lifecycle traditions; the champion-challenger practice where candidate models face production comparison before replacement per the deployment literatures; the documentation-and-reproducibility standards where feature definitions, training data vintages, hyperparameters, and evaluation results persist for audit and rebuild per the MLOps traditions of the operations section); and the interpretation-and-communication practice (the explainability-toolkit literacy where importance rankings, dependence plots, and case-level explanations make ensemble outputs discussable with business stakeholders per the interpretability literatures; the insight-extraction discipline where models surface actionable patterns — which features drive response, which segments behave differently — beyond raw scores per the analytics traditions; the confidence-communication standards where prediction uncertainty and validity limits accompany every deployed score per the communication traditions of the statistics section) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the label-sloppy modeler whose conversion definition mixes attribution windows and bot traffic, training models on fiction, remediated by the target-definition discipline; the leakage-blind-builder whose future-information features produce impossible offline accuracy that collapses in production, remediated by the temporal-split architecture; the accuracy-theater presenter whose headline metric omits baseline comparison and imbalance context, remediated by the evaluation discipline; the imbalance-ignoring optimizer whose ninety-nine-percent accuracy model catches none of the rare converters, remediated by the metrics suite; the calibration-neglecting ranker whose uncalibrated scores enter bid mathematics producing systematic overpayment, remediated by the calibration practice; the deploy-and-forget operator whose model decays silently through drift until spend efficiency collapse forces discovery, remediated by the lifecycle management; and the black-box-defender whose unexplainable model loses stakeholder trust at the first contradicting quarter, remediated by the interpretation practice with detection methods as the diagnostic.
