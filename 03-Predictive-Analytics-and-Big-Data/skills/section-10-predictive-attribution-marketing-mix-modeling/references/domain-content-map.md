## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the measurement discipline that answers advertising's hardest commercial question — which spend actually caused which outcomes — the analytic backbone of budget accountability: the attribution-problem foundation (the causality-gap statement where observed conversions after ad exposure conflate advertising effect with organic demand, selection bias, and coincidence, the gap every measurement method attacks from a different angle per the effectiveness literatures; the measurement-triad framework where attribution modeling, controlled experimentation, and marketing-mix modeling constitute complementary approaches whose triangulation beats any single method per the triad traditions; the last-click-illusion documentation where path-based credit assignment reflects journey observation rather than causal contribution, systematically overcrediting closing channels and undercrediting demand creation per the attribution-critique literatures); the attribution-modeling system (the rule-based-model inventory where last-click, first-click, linear, time-decay, and position rules allocate credit by convention rather than evidence per the rules literatures; the data-driven-attribution reality where algorithmic models including Shapley-value approaches distribute credit by observed marginal contribution with the platform-implementation opacity and identifier dependence documented per the DDA literatures; the identity-and-signal-degradation impact where privacy transformation compresses observable journeys, making user-level attribution structurally incomplete per the transformation literatures of the landscape section; the attribution-appropriate-use discipline where models serve channel-comparison and journey-optimization heuristics rather than budget-truth claims per the interpretation literatures); the experimentation system (the randomized-controlled-trial standard where user-level exposure randomization against holdout measures causal lift, the gold standard whose platform-availability, cost, and scale constraints define its use per the experiment literatures of the behavioral companion; the ghost-ads-and-intent-to-treat designs where eligible-but-unexposed comparisons solve the selection problem of naive exposure analysis per the ad-experiment traditions; the geo-experimental methodology where region-level randomization and matched-market designs measure lift when user-level randomization is impossible, with the power and contamination challenges documented per the geo literatures; the conversion-lift-and-brand-lift-study practice where platform and third-party studies quantify response and attitudinal effects with the vendor-conflict and design-scrutiny disciplines per the study literatures); the marketing-mix-modeling system (the MMM-architecture where aggregate time-series regression decomposes sales into base, media-response with saturation and carryover transformations, promotion, price, seasonality, and control variables per the MMM literatures including the open-source framework traditions of Robyn and Meridian; the response-curve-estimation craft where diminishing-returns saturation and adstock decay parameters shape budget-optimization outputs with the identifiability challenges when channels correlate documented per the curve literatures; the calibration-with-experiments practice where lift-study results anchor or adjust MMM channel coefficients, the hybrid methodology that corrects each method's weakness per the calibration traditions; the privacy-era-advantage literacy where aggregate-data modeling thrives exactly where user-level attribution degrades, explaining MMM's renaissance per the industry literatures; the limitation-honesty discipline where collinearity, aggregation bias, omitted variables, and slow iteration bound MMM claims per the critique literatures); the incrementality-integration practice (the incrementality-first-budgeting philosophy where spend allocation follows measured causal contribution rather than platform-reported attribution, the strategic posture per the incrementality literatures; the always-on-measurement architecture where rolling holdouts, permanent test cells, and continuous geo-experiments institutionalize causality checking per the measurement-ops traditions; the platform-versus-independent-measurement judgment where vendor self-reporting faces conflict-of-interest discount and third-party verification per the independence literatures; the organic-versus-paid disentanglement where brand-search, direct-traffic, and dark-social conversion growth gets correctly credited per the entanglement literatures); and the decision-integration discipline (the budget-optimization translation where response curves, lift estimates, and forecast models combine into allocation recommendations with the diminishing-returns reality per the optimization literatures; the measurement-maturity-progressions where organizations advance from last-click reporting through multi-method triangulation to experiment-institutionalized practice per the maturity traditions; the stakeholder-communication craft where causality uncertainty translates into budget-confidence language executives can act on per the communication traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the last-click-launderer whose budget decisions dress platform attribution reports in causal language no journey model deserves, remediated by the illusion literacy; the model-only-measurer whose MMM never faces experimental calibration while collinear channels trade credit arbitrarily, remediated by the hybrid practice; the experiment-elitist whose gold-standard purism delays all measurement because user-level RCTs are unavailable, ignoring geo and hybrid alternatives, remediated by the triad framework; the vendor-report-acceptor whose platform lift studies face no design scrutiny despite obvious self-interest, remediated by the independence judgment; the one-study-budgeter whose single experiment permanently fixes allocation despite changing conditions and saturation dynamics, remediated by the always-on architecture; and the attribution-only-optimizer whose channel-level credit tuning proceeds while total-advertising incrementality goes unmeasured, remediated by the incrementality-first philosophy with detection methods as the diagnostic.
