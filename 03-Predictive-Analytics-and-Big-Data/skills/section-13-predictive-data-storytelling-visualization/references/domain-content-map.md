## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the communication discipline whose craft determines whether valid analysis influences decisions or dies in decks: the visualization-design foundation (the perceptual-priority principle where encoding choices follow human visual-processing hierarchy — position and length read accurately, area and angle mislead — per the graphics literatures including the Cleveland-McGill effectiveness ordering; the chart-selection discipline where comparison, composition, distribution, relationship, and change-over-time questions map to specific chart families with the default-recommendation practice of the design traditions; the decluttering-and-emphasis craft where non-data ink reduction, direct labeling, and strategic color use direct attention per the Tufte and Few traditions of the design sources; the honesty-standards discipline where axis treatment, truncation, scale selection, and window choice face the integrity requirements connecting to the statistical-communication practice of the foundations section); the dashboard-design practice (the information-hierarchy architecture where glance-layer indicators, monitoring-layer trends, and investigation-layer detail organize dashboards by cognitive load per the dashboard literatures; the comparison-context principle where every metric displays against target, prior period, or benchmark because isolated numbers carry no meaning per the context traditions; the interactivity-design judgment where filters, drill-down, and time selection serve analyst exploration without enabling definition drift per the self-service boundaries of the operations section; the audience-fitting discipline where executive, operational, and analyst dashboards differ in density, refresh, and vocabulary per the hierarchy principle of the operations section); the narrative-structure craft (the answer-first-pyramid where conclusions lead and support follows, inverting the analysis chronology that buries findings per the business-communication traditions; the situation-complication-resolution arc where current state, tension, and recommended action structure analytic stories per the storytelling literatures; the so-what-chain discipline where every exhibited number connects to a decision implication or gets cut per the actionability traditions; the uncertainty-narrative integration where confidence, ranges, and caveats enter the story as credibility builders rather than appendices per the communication standards of the statistics section); the audience-adaptation practice (the executive-briefing craft where one-page summaries, decision framing, and recommendation clarity serve time-constrained leadership per the executive-communication traditions; the cross-functional-translation where statistical concepts convert into marketing vocabulary without distortion, the bilingual skill of the analytics professional per the translation traditions; the skeptic-handling preparation where methodology challenges, alternative explanations, and conflicting-data questions receive anticipated answers per the defense traditions; the visual-literacy-calibration where audience chart familiarity determines complexity tolerance per the literacy traditions); the campaign-readout specialization (the standard-readout architecture where objective, spend, delivery, response, incrementality evidence, learning, and recommendation organize post-campaign analysis per the measurement traditions of the attribution section; the multi-source-reconciliation presentation where platform, attribution, MMM, and experimental results display together with method-specific confidence rather than false reconciliation per the triad traditions; the learning-extraction discipline where readouts accumulate validated patterns into the insight repositories of the operations section per the knowledge traditions; the recommendation-craft where analytic findings convert into specific budget, creative, targeting, and testing actions with expected impact per the decision-support traditions); and the tooling-and-production practice (the visualization-toolkit literacy where Python and R graphics libraries, BI-platform visuals, and presentation tools serve different fidelity and iteration needs per the tooling traditions; the template-and-style-system development where organizational visual standards accelerate production and build recognition per the design-system traditions; the automation-integration where scheduled report generation connects to the operations pipelines per the automation practice) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the chart-junk-producer whose three-dimensional effects, gradient fills, and decorative elements distort perception while signaling effort, remediated by the honesty and decluttering standards; the burial-analyst whose forty-slide chronology places the recommendation after the appendix, losing the decision audience at slide three, remediated by the answer-first structure; the number-dump reporter whose tables of uncontextualized metrics delegate interpretation to time-poor stakeholders, remediated by the comparison-context principle; the uncertainty-concealer whose precise-looking point forecasts hide distributions that would have prevented overcommitment, remediated by the uncertainty-narrative integration; the audience-monotone presenter whose identical deck faces the CMO and the analytics team, missing both, remediated by the adaptation practice; and the readout-without-recommendation analyst whose campaign reviews describe but never direct, remediated by the recommendation craft with detection methods as the diagnostic.
