## Exercise 1 — Forecast demand through framework foundations (object specification
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Predictive Analytics & Big Data scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** forecast demand through framework foundations (object specification, decomposition mindset, uncertainty primacy, judgment roles), the method toolkit (benchmarks, exponential smoothing, regression with predictors, ML methods, hierarchical reconciliation), marketing specifics (promotion effects, campaign lift, new products, external drivers), evaluation discipline (metric selection, backtesting, bias monitoring, value assessment), operational practice (cadence discipline, scenario architecture, communication standards, feedback loops), and big-data enhancement (nowcasting, granular scale, signal integration), avoiding sophistication chasing, point-estimate planning, promotion blindness, override culture, in-sample admiration.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.
