## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the forecasting discipline that executes the course description's central promise — anticipating market demand — through time-series science and judgment integration: the forecasting-framework foundation (the forecast-object specification where the unit, horizon, granularity, and frequency of prediction get fixed before method selection because monthly-national and daily-SKU forecasts demand different machinery per the forecasting literatures; the decomposition-mindset where series structure splits into trend, seasonality, cycles, and irregular components whose identification guides method choice per the decomposition traditions of the trend section; the uncertainty-primacy principle where the forecast distribution matters more than the point estimate for inventory, budget, and capacity decisions, making interval quality the real deliverable per the probabilistic-forecasting literatures; the judgment-role clarity where statistical forecasts and human adjustment combine with the documented evidence that structured judgment integration outperforms either alone while unstructured override typically degrades accuracy per the judgment literatures); the method toolkit (the benchmark-family discipline where naive, seasonal-naive, and average methods set the accuracy floor every sophisticated model must beat per the benchmark traditions; the exponential-smoothing family where weighted-history methods with trend and seasonal extensions handle large numbers of series efficiently per the smoothing literatures; the regression-with-predictors approach where promotional calendars, price changes, economic indicators, weather, and leading signals enter as explanatory variables with the causality-claim caution of the statistics section; the modern-machine-learning methods where gradient-boosted and deep-learning forecasters add value on rich feature sets with the data-hunger and overfitting discipline documented per the ML-forecasting literatures; the hierarchical-forecasting problem where SKU, category, and total forecasts must reconcile because independent predictions at multiple levels contradict per the reconciliation literatures); the marketing-demand specifics (the promotion-effects modeling where discount and campaign impacts enter forecasts with the post-promotion-dip and pull-forward dynamics that naive models miss per the promotion literatures of the behavioral companion; the campaign-lift-forecasting where planned media activity predicts demand increments using historical response and the reach-frequency modeling of the media companion; the new-product-forecasting challenge where absent history demands analogy-based methods, pre-launch signal use including search and social indicators of the trend section, and explicit wide uncertainty per the new-product literatures; the external-driver integration where economic conditions, competitive launches, platform algorithm changes, and cultural events enter as scenario variables per the driver literatures); the forecast-evaluation discipline (the error-metric selection where scale-dependent, percentage, and distributional metrics suit different decision contexts with the zero-and-small-value hazards of naive percentage errors documented per the evaluation literatures; the backtesting methodology where rolling-origin evaluation across history simulates real forecasting conditions including the information actually available per the backtesting traditions; the bias-and-tracking-signal monitoring where systematic over or under prediction receives detection and correction distinct from random error per the bias literatures; the forecast-value assessment where accuracy improvements convert into decision-value estimates covering inventory, budget, and staffing because accuracy for its own sake lacks commercial meaning per the value traditions); the operational-forecasting practice (the cadence-and-rolling discipline where forecasts refresh on schedules matched to decision cycles with the horizon sliding forward per the planning traditions; the scenario-and-sensitivity architecture where alternative demand paths under promotional, competitive, and macro scenarios support robust planning per the scenario literatures; the forecast-communication standards where ranges, assumptions, and risk factors accompany numbers in planning meetings per the communication traditions of the storytelling section; the feedback-loop closure where realized demand returns into model retraining and judgment calibration per the learning traditions); and the big-data-enhancement frontier (the nowcasting-techniques where high-frequency signals including search, traffic, and transaction streams estimate current demand state between official statistics per the nowcasting literatures of the alternative-data traditions; the granular-forecasting scale where machine learning enables SKU-store-day predictions whose aggregation and reconciliation discipline becomes the binding constraint per the scale literatures; the signal-integration-modeling where the trend-detection outputs feed as leading indicators with the validation discipline that spurious leading series demand per the integration traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the sophistication-chaser whose complex models lose to seasonal-naive benchmarks because evaluation never included them, remediated by the benchmark discipline; the point-estimate-planner whose single-number forecasts drive inventory decisions that distributions would have protected, remediated by the uncertainty primacy; the promotion-blind-forecaster whose campaign-period predictions ignore lift, cannibalization, and post-promotion dip, remediated by the marketing-specifics modeling; the override-culture victim whose executive gut adjustments systematically degrade statistical accuracy without tracking, remediated by the judgment-integration discipline; the in-sample-admirer whose model selection rests on fitted history rather than rolling-origin backtests, remediated by the evaluation methodology; and the communicate-and-forget forecaster whose predictions never face realized-demand reconciliation, freezing error unexamined, remediated by the feedback-loop closure with detection methods as the diagnostic.
