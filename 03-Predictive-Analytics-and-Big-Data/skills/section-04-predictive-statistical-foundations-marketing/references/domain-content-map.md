## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the statistical literacy whose absence converts data analysis into pattern superstition, the ground floor every modeling section builds on: the data-and-distribution foundation (the measurement-scale literacy where nominal, ordinal, interval, and ratio data determine permissible operations and appropriate methods per the measurement traditions; the distributional-shape awareness where skew, modality, and outlier structure in marketing data — conversion rates, order values, engagement times — govern method selection because normality assumptions routinely fail per the distribution literatures; the descriptive-statistics discipline where central tendency, spread, and quantile summaries precede any inference, with the mean-versus-median judgment under skew constituting basic numeracy per the summary traditions; the sampling-and-population logic where platform users, survey respondents, and tracked visitors differ systematically from target populations, the bias inheritance documented in the landscape section requiring explicit acknowledgment per the sampling literatures); the inference-and-uncertainty core (the confidence-interval habit where every reported metric carries uncertainty bounds whose width reflects sample size and variance per the estimation traditions; the hypothesis-testing mechanics where significance tests answer whether observed differences exceed noise with the p-value interpretation discipline that avoids both significance-worship and significance-ignoring per the testing literatures; the multiple-comparisons problem where testing many segments, variants, and metrics inflates false discoveries, demanding correction or preregistered focus per the comparison literatures connecting to the experimentation practice; the power-and-sample-size literacy where small samples cannot detect real effects, making underpowered analysis a source of missed truth rather than neutral caution per the design traditions); the relationship-analysis toolkit (the correlation-versus-causation discipline where association measures quantify co-movement while causal claims require design — experiments, natural experiments, or rigorous quasi-methods — the distinction the attribution and incrementality sections operationalize per the causal literatures; the confounding-and-simpson awareness where lurking variables and segment-mix shifts reverse aggregate associations, the classic marketing-data trap where campaign audiences differ from non-audiences by selection per the confounding literatures; the regression-foundation where linear and logistic models quantify relationships while controlling covariates, the workhorse connecting to the predictive-modeling section per the regression traditions; the time-dependence awareness where autocorrelation, seasonality, and trend violate independence assumptions, motivating the forecasting methods of the later section per the series literatures); the probabilistic-thinking upgrade (the base-rate discipline where conversion probabilities condition on segment priors rather than headline metrics, connecting to the Bayesian intuition per the base-rate literatures of the behavioral companion course; the conditional-probability fluency where funnel analysis correctly distinguishes conversion-given-exposure from exposure-given-conversion reasoning per the funnel literatures; the expected-value thinking where decision evaluation integrates probability and magnitude rather than optimizing single outcomes per the decision traditions; the regression-to-the-mean awareness where extreme performers — best audiences, worst days — revert naturally, preventing both premature celebration and panic per the reversion literatures); the statistical-communication practice (the uncertainty-language standards where probably, likely, and significant carry disciplined meaning in reports rather than decorative emphasis per the communication traditions; the chart-honesty discipline where axis truncation, dual-scale deception, and cherry-picked windows face the visualization standards of the storytelling section; the effect-size-emphasis practice where practical magnitude outranks statistical significance in business reporting per the significance traditions of the experimentation companion); and the tooling connection (the statistical-environment literacy where Python and R libraries implement the methods with the understanding requirement that tool fluency never substitutes for conceptual mastery per the data-science traditions) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the mean-reporter whose skewed order-value averages misrepresent typical customers while medians and distributions go uncomputed, remediated by the distributional discipline; the significance-fetishist whose p-value worship treats 0.049 and 0.051 as different truths while ignoring effect size and power, remediated by the testing literacy; the causal-claim-slipper whose correlation findings get presented as campaign effects without design support, remediated by the causation discipline; the segment-dredging fisherman whose twenty subgroup comparisons inevitably surface false winners that scale into wasted budget, remediated by the multiple-comparisons correction; the base-rate-ignoring targeter whose rare-event response rates get read without prior context, remediated by the probabilistic upgrade; and the uncertainty-hiding reporter whose precise-looking dashboards conceal sampling error and confidence ranges, remediated by the communication standards with detection methods as the diagnostic.
