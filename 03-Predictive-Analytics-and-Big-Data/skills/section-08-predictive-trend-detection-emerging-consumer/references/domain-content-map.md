## Unit 1 — Unit 1

**Scope:** Covers the trend-intelligence discipline whose early detection of emerging consumer behavior delivers the course description's promise of spotting trends and forecasting demand before they happen: the trend-concept foundation (the trend-versus-fad distinction where sustained directional shifts in behavior, need, or value differ from brief spikes, requiring duration and diffusion evidence before strategic response per the trend literatures; the signal-hierarchy framework where weak signals accumulate from fringe behavior through early-adopter uptake to mainstream visibility, with detection value inversely proportional to obviousness per the weak-signal traditions; the diffusion-curve literacy where innovation adoption follows documented S-curve patterns whose early segments predict later scale per the diffusion traditions; the trend-mechanism awareness where technological enablement, economic pressure, cultural shift, regulatory change, and generational replacement generate trends whose causal engine determines durability per the driver literatures); the data-signal inventory (the search-behavior-signals where query volume trajectories reveal need emergence with the documented leading properties and the normalization and seasonality controls required per the search-data literatures; the social-conversation-signals where volume, velocity, sentiment, and network structure on platforms indicate cultural momentum with the bot, platform-shift, and echo-chamber contamination managed per the listening literatures; the transaction-and-basket-signals where purchase composition shifts, new-product velocity, and category cross-purchase patterns reveal revealed-preference trends per the retail-data traditions; the content-and-media-signals where streaming, publication, and creator-content patterns indicate attention migration per the media literatures; the review-and-community-signals where language evolution, complaint themes, and enthusiast-community activity surface unmet needs per the voice-of-customer traditions); the detection-methodology toolkit (the time-series-decomposition methods where trend, seasonal, and residual components separate so directional signals escape seasonal confusion per the decomposition traditions of the forecasting section; the anomaly-and-change-point detection where statistical methods flag distribution breaks whose investigation separates genuine emergence from data artifact per the changepoint literatures; the velocity-and-acceleration metrics where growth-rate dynamics distinguish linear drift from exponential emergence per the growth literatures; the network-diffusion analysis where adoption spread patterns across communities indicate contagion potential per the diffusion-analytics traditions; the text-and-topic-evolution methods where language change in reviews, posts, and queries tracks concept emergence per the text-analytics traditions); the analysis-and-validation discipline (the triangulation requirement where candidate trends face corroboration across independent signal sources because single-platform spikes routinely prove artifactual per the validation traditions; the base-rate-and-denominator discipline where raw volume growth without market-share context misreads category-wide seasonality as trend per the normalization literatures; the causal-mechanism hypothesis practice where durable trend claims identify the underlying driver rather than resting on correlation of rising series per the mechanism traditions of the behavioral companion; the prediction-track-record building where trend forecasts get documented and scored against outcomes, accumulating the calibration evidence that separates intelligence from opinion per the forecasting-accountability traditions); the commercial-translation practice (the opportunity-sizing methodology where trend trajectories convert into addressable-market estimates with assumption transparency per the sizing traditions; the speed-versus-confidence judgment where early trend action carries higher uncertainty cost while late action forfeits advantage, the decision framework per the timing literatures; the campaign-translation craft where trend insights become positioning, creative themes, product recommendations, and audience strategies per the strategy literatures; the monitoring-dashboard integration where trend indicators enter continuous tracking rather than one-off reports per the operations traditions); and the trend-intelligence ethics (the manipulation-boundary discipline where exploiting insecurity-driven trends including body-image and financial-anxiety waves faces the ethics frameworks of the behavioral companion per the vulnerability literatures; the privacy-boundary awareness where trend detection from aggregate signals differs ethically from individual surveillance per the governance frameworks of the quality section) together with the section anti-patterns — the failure library: the spike-chaser whose strategy pivots on single-platform volume bursts that seasonal normalization would have explained, remediated by the decomposition discipline; the single-source-believer whose social-listening trend never faced transaction or search corroboration before budget commitment, remediated by the triangulation requirement; the mechanism-blind-surfacer whose trend report catalogs rising series without identifying the driver that determines durability, remediated by the hypothesis practice; the denominator-ignorant celebrator whose fifty-percent growth on tiny base gets treated as market shift, remediated by the base-rate discipline; the fad-investor whose category-scale commitment follows what diffusion analysis would have classified as early spike and decay, remediated by the curve literacy; and the unaccountable-forecaster whose trend predictions never face scored follow-up, allowing confident error to persist uncorrected, remediated by the track-record discipline with detection methods as the diagnostic.
