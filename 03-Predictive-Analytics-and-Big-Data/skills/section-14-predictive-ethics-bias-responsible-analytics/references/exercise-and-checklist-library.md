# Part A: Practical Exercises

## Exercise 1 — Practice responsible analytics through bias taxonomy (data channels
**Scenario:** provide a realistic Predictive Analytics & Big Data scenario appropriate to the audience (real or closely modeled on documented practice).
**Objective:** practice responsible analytics through bias taxonomy (data channels, proxy mechanics, feedback loops, objective mis-specification), the advertising harm landscape (discriminatory targeting history, vulnerability boundaries, manipulation optimization, information-environment effects), fairness assessment (metric literacy, impact testing, documentation standards, mitigation toolkits), responsible-AI governance (risk management, human oversight, explainability obligations, AI-use policy), privacy ethics (minimization, secondary use, re-identification risk, consumer expectations), and professional culture (ethics review, dissent channels, industry participation, trust accounting), avoiding bias-blind deployment, proxy laundering, loop ignorance, metric monoculture, explainability dodging, and minimization scoffing failures.
**Constraints:** must use the framework taught in the corresponding unit; must state assumptions and trade-offs; one deliverable, length-bounded.
**Deliverable:** the professional artifact this capability produces (memo, table, plan, analysis, or model — as applicable).
**Evaluation criteria:** correct application of the framework; decisions justified with evidence; limitations honestly stated; artifact usable by a colleague without further explanation.

## Exercise 2 — Integrated capstone
**Scenario:** one realistic problem spanning the whole section.
**Objective:** take the problem through every unit's framework in sequence.
**Constraints:** all unit artifacts required and internally consistent; evidence discipline maintained throughout.
**Deliverable:** a coherent campaign/portfolio document assembled as real professional documentation.
**Evaluation criteria:** consistency across artifacts; each capability demonstrably exercised; trade-offs stated at each decision point.
